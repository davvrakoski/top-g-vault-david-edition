# Advanced Map Readability & Navigation

Players should be able to form a mental map without staring at a minimap every second.

## Tools
Landmarks, district color/material identity, sightline framing, lighting, signage, road hierarchy, audio landmarks and objective silhouettes.

## Route design
Provide primary routes, shortcuts and optional exploration loops. Dead ends should usually contain a reward, story beat or tactical purpose.

## Density
High detail is not the same as high information. Too many equally loud props make every area look the same.

## Lab
Remove the HUD and ask testers to travel between three major locations. Note where they get lost and what landmark was missing.
