# Overgrown Abandoned Interior — Ruin + Nature Recipe

The abandoned interior references combine **structural storytelling**, controlled clutter, vegetation invasion and directional sunlight. The strongest effect is the contrast between dark neglected interior space and bright natural light entering through damage.

## Layer the scene
1. clean base building,
2. structural damage: missing roof panels, broken railings, damaged walls,
3. large debris: furniture, panels, beams,
4. dirt/grime material pass,
5. vegetation growth from believable moisture/light entry points,
6. small clutter,
7. dust/ambient particles,
8. audio and gameplay dressing.

## Believability rules
Plants should favor openings, cracks, wet zones and sunlit areas. Debris should show where it fell from. Broken architecture needs a structural reason instead of random holes everywhere.

## Lighting
Use sunlight shafts and bright exterior openings as focal tools. Atmosphere/post effects should support the scene but not turn every bright opening into white bloom.

## Gameplay
Keep the route readable through clutter. Color/value separation should reveal walkable spaces, danger zones and interactables.

## Performance
Many ruin props can be static/anchored. Use pre-broken chunks instead of simulating hundreds of physical fragments. Decorative dust and falling particles should be local and low-rate.
