# Low-Poly Village Kit — Clean Stylized Production

The low-poly village reference shows that simple geometry can look professional when **shape language, palette and composition are consistent**.

## Kit pieces
- wall modules,
- roof variants,
- window/door modules,
- fence pieces,
- trees,
- barrels/crates,
- road/path pieces,
- rocks,
- market props,
- landmark tower.

## Style rules
Choose a limited palette and repeat proportions. Roof angles, trim thickness, tree shape and edge softness should feel like the same world. Do not mix photoreal PBR props into a deliberately simple low-poly town unless there is a strong reason.

## Geometry
Use enough polygons to preserve the silhouette. A low-poly object is not automatically optimized if you place 20,000 individual Parts. Prefer sensible MeshParts/modules and reusable content.

## Lighting
Soft clean lighting usually fits this style better than heavy bloom, grain and extreme contrast.

## Production benefit
A strong low-poly kit is fast to expand: one village kit can generate houses, shops, alleys and plazas without hundreds of unique assets.
