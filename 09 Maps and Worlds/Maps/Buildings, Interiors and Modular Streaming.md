# Buildings, Interiors & Modular Streaming

Enterable buildings can destroy performance if every interior is permanently loaded and simulated.

## Modular kit
Create wall, floor, ceiling, doorway, trim and prop modules that snap to a consistent grid. Reuse materials and texture sets.

## Interior activation
Separate visual existence from simulation. A distant building may exist visually while interior AI, interactables and detailed VFX sleep until relevant.

## Navigation
Every enterable structure needs readable entrances, exits and vertical circulation. Test NPC navigation separately from player traversal.

## Lab
Build one repeated storefront shell with three different interior layouts using the same kit. Compare memory against three fully unique buildings.
