# Vibrant Fantasy Biome — Saturated Without Becoming Noise

The colorful fantasy environment uses high saturation, but it still reads because the values and focal colors are organized.

## Palette hierarchy
Pick:
- 1 dominant environmental family,
- 1 secondary family,
- 1 accent color for gameplay/focal VFX.

Do not make every object maximum saturation. If everything glows, nothing is important.

## Scene layers
Use strong tree/rock silhouettes, clean path shapes, bright water/waterfall focal points, and a limited number of magical particles. Let geometry carry most of the scene.

## Lighting
Use color correction carefully. Preserve readable skin/UI/enemy colors. Use bloom only for genuinely bright/emissive elements.

## Waterfall focal point
A waterfall can combine a shaped mesh/beam/particle layer, splash particles and audio. Keep the expensive layers local to the camera/player rather than filling the map.

## Optimization
Fantasy art often gets expensive from transparent foliage + particles + lights. Establish a visual budget per biome.
