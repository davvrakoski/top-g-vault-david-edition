# Vegetation, Grass, and Trees Blueprint

Vegetation can destroy performance through repetition, transparency, shadows, and unique materials.

Use:
- reusable variants
- distance LOD/render fidelity
- restrained transparent leaf layering
- shadow policy by distance/importance
- collision only where gameplay needs it
- grouped placement tools/procedural distribution
- biome palettes so variation feels intentional

Do not make every blade of grass a physical Part.
