# Lake, Dock & Stylized Water Scene

The lake reference works because the dock creates a strong line toward the distant house/landmark. Water is visually calm and the world uses large simple shapes.

## Water design
Use Terrain water for the base when it fits the style. Tune color/transparency/reflection properties to match the palette. Add shoreline vegetation and rocks to hide hard transitions.

## Dock
Make the dock modular. Use a small number of plank meshes/parts, simple collision, and avoid unnecessary physics on every board.

## Composition
Place a destination across the water. The dock should visually point to it. Use foreground plants to frame the view without covering the route.

## Boats
If decorative, anchor them and use a subtle scripted/constraint bob only if the effect is visible. If drivable, treat them as a vehicle system with network ownership and server validation rather than decorative physics.

## Performance
Water and transparency can already be visually busy. Do not stack excessive translucent foam, mist and particles across the whole lake.
