# Stylized & Anime Lighting

Stylized lighting benefits from deliberate color/value grouping, clean key lights and controlled effects.

## Anime-like approach
Favor readable shadow regions, selective saturation, strong silhouette and art-directed highlights. Avoid mixing photoreal roughness/noise with flat stylized characters unless the contrast is intentional.

## Lab
Create three presets — noon, sunset, night — with the same character and verify skin/clothing remain readable.
