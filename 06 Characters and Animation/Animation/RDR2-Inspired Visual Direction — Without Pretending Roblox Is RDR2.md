# RDR2-Inspired Visual Direction — Without Pretending Roblox Is RDR2

Study visual principles instead of trying to reproduce another engine exactly.

Use:
- natural material roughness
- restrained saturation
- atmospheric depth
- warm/cool time-of-day contrast
- strong environmental composition
- believable clutter density
- animation weight
- layered ambience
- weather transitions

Avoid:
- overbloom
- fake 4K noise on every surface
- pitch-black shadows
- hyperreal character textures next to simple Roblox geometry

The target is "grounded and cinematic within Roblox," not a misleading claim of literal RDR2 rendering.
