# Anime Roblox Visual Direction

Anime style benefits from clarity rather than raw shader complexity.

Priorities:
- clean silhouettes
- controlled color palette
- face readability
- deliberate key/rim lighting
- stylized VFX shapes
- strong value grouping
- consistent outline strategy if used

Do not mix photoreal PBR dirt, flat anime faces, neon UI, and realistic gore unless the contrast is an intentional art direction.
