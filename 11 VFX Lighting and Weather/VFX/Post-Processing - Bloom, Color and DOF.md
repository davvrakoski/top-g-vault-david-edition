# Post-Processing: Bloom, Color & DOF

Post-processing should finish a scene, not rescue bad lighting.

Use bloom sparingly around truly bright sources. Color correction should support the palette without crushing blacks or clipping highlights. Depth-of-field can be cinematic but may reduce gameplay readability and should be used cautiously.

## Lab
Toggle every post effect off. If the scene collapses, improve the base lighting/materials first.
