# Thunder, Lightning & Accessibility

Lightning needs anticipation, a short visual flash/bolt and thunder delayed by distance. Random timing should feel natural without rapid flashing.

## Accessibility
Provide a reduced-flash option and avoid repeated high-intensity full-screen flashes. Critical gameplay information should never depend only on the flash.

## Lab
Test storms with flash intensity at 100%, reduced mode and low graphics settings.
