# Day/Night and Lighting Blueprint

Centralize time-of-day control.

Profile contains:
- clock time
- brightness/exposure
- atmosphere/fog
- sky/clouds
- color correction
- ambience audio
- light activation rules

Use smooth transitions and cap dynamic lights. Test readability during darkest conditions; "realistic" darkness that hides gameplay is usually poor UX.
