# LIGHTING, ATMOSPHERE & GRAPHICS

=================================================

Roblox lighting tools include:
- Lighting properties
- Atmosphere
- Sky
- Clouds
- PointLight
- SpotLight
- SurfaceLight
- Bloom
- Color correction
- Depth of field
- blur
- sun rays and other supported effects

DO NOT SAY:
"Enable Lumen in Roblox."
Lumen is an Unreal Engine system.

REALISTIC LIGHTING CHECKLIST:
- choose one visual mood
- decide sun direction
- create readable shadow shapes
- avoid crushed blacks
- avoid nuclear bloom
- avoid oversaturation
- use fog/haze for depth
- limit dynamic lights
- keep important gameplay targets readable
- test day/night separately
- test low graphics settings

ANIME LIGHTING:
- simpler color blocks
- strong key light
- controlled outlines if style supports it
- saturated accents
- clean value separation
- avoid random photoreal details fighting stylization

RDR2-LIKE GOAL IN ROBLOX:
Do not try to literally clone RDR2 rendering.
Study the ingredients:
- natural color palette
- atmospheric perspective
- warm/cool time-of-day balance
- layered fog
- strong landscape composition
- grounded materials
- restrained post-processing
- convincing animation
- environmental audio
- believable weather
Then build a Roblox-appropriate interpretation.
