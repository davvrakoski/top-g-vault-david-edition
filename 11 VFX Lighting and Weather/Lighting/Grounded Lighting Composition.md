# Grounded Lighting Composition

Grounded lighting uses believable direction, controlled contrast and atmosphere rather than maximum brightness and bloom.

## Compose
Choose the key light direction to support navigation and focal points. Use shadow shapes to separate spaces. Keep enemies/interactables readable against backgrounds.

## Lab
Screenshot the same scene in grayscale. If the subject disappears into the background, fix value separation before adding color effects.
