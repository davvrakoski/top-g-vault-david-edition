# Environment Lighting — Stylized to Realistic

The reference set spans clean stylized worlds and realistic industrial/ruin scenes. The engine tools can be similar; **art direction changes how they are used**.

## Stylized lighting
- simple broad key light,
- clean shadow shapes,
- bright but controlled sky fill,
- limited post-processing,
- strong color separation.

## Grounded/realistic lighting
- believable sun/key direction,
- more restrained saturation,
- roughness/material response matters more,
- atmosphere provides depth,
- local lights need physical motivation,
- exposure should preserve detail in dark and bright areas.

## Interior/exterior transition
When moving from a dark warehouse/ruin to bright outdoors, avoid instant unreadable exposure shifts. Tune lighting/post effects for gameplay readability.

## Roblox tool stack
Lighting service + Atmosphere + Sky + Clouds + local lights + post-processing such as bloom/color correction/depth-of-field where appropriate.

## Rule
Do not use post-processing to repair bad materials or composition. Fix geometry/material/value structure first.
