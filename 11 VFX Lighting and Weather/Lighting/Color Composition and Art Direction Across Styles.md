# Color Composition & Art Direction Across Styles

The references range from white/blue snow to saturated fantasy to brown industrial realism. A professional vault needs to explain **why palettes work**.

## Palette system
Define:
- environment base colors,
- architecture colors,
- character colors,
- enemy/danger colors,
- interactable colors,
- VFX element colors,
- UI accent colors.

## Avoid palette collisions
If interactables glow cyan, do not make every decorative lamp and waterfall use the same cyan intensity. Gameplay language needs protected colors/values.

## Warm/cool structure
Warm light against cool shadows creates depth. Cool environments can use warm focal accents. Stylized art can exaggerate this; realistic art should keep material response believable.

## Saturation budget
Reserve maximum saturation for a few focal elements. Large environment surfaces generally need room for VFX/UI/characters to stand out.

## Consistency
A scene can use low-poly geometry and still have premium lighting. It can use realistic meshes and still look cheap if every asset comes from a different art style.
