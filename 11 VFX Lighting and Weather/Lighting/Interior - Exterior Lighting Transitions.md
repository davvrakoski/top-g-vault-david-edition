# Interior/Exterior Lighting Transitions

Moving through doors and windows exposes large brightness differences. Design interior ambience, local lights, atmosphere and exposure-like presentation so the player can still read the scene.

Avoid solving every dark corner with a real-time light. Emissive materials, baked art choices and local contrast can sell the space more cheaply.

## Lab
Walk repeatedly from bright outdoors into the darkest interior while fighting. Tune readability, not just screenshots.
