*An overview of creator assets and the Creator Store.*

## Create and sell Studio plugins

You can create and sell Studio [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Studio/Studio plugins|plugins]] to the creator community through the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Creator Store|Creator Store]]. A plugin is an extension that adds additional features or functionality to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Studio/Roblox Studio|Roblox Studio]] and helps improve creator workflows. You can either offer plugins for free or sell them for a minimum of \$4.99 USD. Roblox offers a market-leading revenue share for these sales, as only taxes and payment processing fees are deducted. For more information, see [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Creator Store#Distribute and sell assets|Creator Store - Distribute and sell assets]].

Creator assets promote collaboration and resource sharing, and help developers create better and more dynamic games.
