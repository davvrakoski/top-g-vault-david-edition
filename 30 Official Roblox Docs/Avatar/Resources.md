*Get all the resources you need to build characters and accessories.*

The following downloadable files are available for your use:

- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#References|References]] — Finished assets you can use as reference.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#Auto-setup references|Auto-Setup References]] — Assets that meet specific avatar auto-setup requirements.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#Project files|Project Files]] — Fundamental resources like rigs and cages for building your own assets.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#Mannequin models|Mannequins]] — Models you can use as sizing reference.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#Templates|Templates]] — Prebuilt characters that require small additional customization.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Resources#Add-ons and tools|Add-ons and Tools]] — Third-party tools and add-ons.

## References

### Advanced R15 rigs

The following [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications#Advanced R15 rigs|advanced R15 rigs]] include additional bones or joints for a higher level of realism with articulated hands, shoulders, and spine movements.

Mannequin

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of a AA avatar-ready character body, including a dynamic head, fingers, layered accessories, and 2K PBR textures.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/Mannequin-HigherFidelity.zip)

Robuta

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an anime-style, avatar-ready character body, including a dynamic head, fingers, rigid and layered accessory assets, and 2K PBR textures.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/Robuta-HigherFidelity.zip)

HipToBeSquare

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of a blocky-style, avatar-ready character body, including a dynamic head, fingers, rigid and layered accessory assets, and color, roughness, and metal PBR textures.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/HipToBeSquare-HigherFidelity.zip)

Roxie

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an avatar-ready character body, including a dynamic head, fingers, rigid and layered accessory assets, and PBR textures. When you import this model, set **Rig Scale** to **Rthro Narrow**.

<br>

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/Roxie-HigherFidelity.zip)

### Standard R15 rigs

The following [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications#Standard R15 rigs|standard R15 rigs]] include the standard amount of bones or joints. Finished assets like Nature Girl and Stylish Male include clothing and accessory items that may cause validation errors unless they are removed.

Nature Girl

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an avatar-ready character body, including clothing and rigid accessory assets. Each asset includes its own Blender and Maya project file, as well as their respective PBR textures. <br> <br>

If you are opening these files in Blender, the armature bones may display in an unexpected orientation because the source model was originally exported from Maya. This different orientation doesn't affect the performance of the armature rig.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/NatureArcherGirl.zip)

Stylish Male

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an avatar-ready character body, including clothing and rigid accessory assets. Each asset includes its own Blender and Maya project file, as well as their respective PBR textures. <br> <br>

If you are opening these files in Blender, the armature bones may display in an unexpected orientation because the source model was originally exported from Maya. This different orientation doesn't affect the performance of the armature rig.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/StylizedMaleWithClothing.zip)

Fish Person

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A rigged and skinned humanoid character model with a full body cage, facial animation rig, and associated PBR texture maps. <br><br> If this asset were to be uploaded to the Marketplace, the bulb antenna must be a separate head accessory and not part of the character's head geometry.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/avatar/dynamic-heads/reference-files/Fish-Person.zip)

Goblin

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A rigged and skinned humanoid character model with a full body cage, facial animation rig.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/avatar/dynamic-heads/reference-files/GoblinCharacter.zip)

Blocky

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A Blocky character model with an animatable head and a full body cage.

<br>

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/avatar/dynamic-heads/reference-files/BlockyCharacter.fbx)

Lola

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A skinned R15 character created from the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Art/Modeling/Skin a humanoid model|Skinning a Humanoid Model]] guide. Since this reference model doesn't yet have [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications|inner and outer cage mesh data]], this model can't equip rigid or layered accessories.

<br>

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/modeling/skinned-meshes/Lola.fbx)

Tshirt - Uncaged

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Uncaged example clothing ready for caging in a 3D modeling software.

Clothing Examples

Caged 3D accessory models and associated PBR textures. Ready for import into Studio or in a modeling tool.

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar Setup/Avatar Setup|Avatar Auto-Setup]] ready character model, including clothing and rigid accessory assets and PBR texture assets. <br> <br>
This Auto-Setup template is not compatible with the traditional avatar creation workflow.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/NatureArcherGirl-AutoSetup.zip)

Stylish Male - Auto-Setup

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

A comprehensive `.zip` folder of an [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar Setup/Avatar Setup|Avatar Auto-Setup]] ready character model, including the base body and associated PBR textures. <br> <br>
This Auto-Setup template is not compatible with the traditional avatar creation workflow.

[Download](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/reference-files/StylizedMale-AutoSetup.zip)

## Project files

The following are base rig, attachment, and cage files for avatar item creation in third-party applications. Choose the appropriate type of project file for your use-case. These assets are not suitable for direct import into Studio.

Standard R15 Rig and Attachments

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Template for various software that includes a [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications#Standard R15 rigs|standard R15 rig]] and attachments. Use this template for rigging **bodies** and **clothing** items.

[Download (.blend, .ma, .fbx)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/modeling/meshes/reference-files/Rig_and_Attachments_Templates.zip)

Cages for Clothing

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Project files for creation, includes a full-body inner and outer cage mesh for creation of **clothing** items.

[Download (.blend, .ma, .fbx)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/modeling/meshes/reference-files/Clothing_Cage_Templates.zip)

Cages for Bodies

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Project files for creation, includes the 15 individual body part cages required for caging your avatar **bodies**.

[Download (.blend, .ma, .fbx)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/modeling/meshes/reference-files/Body_Cage_Templates.zip)

Combined Project Files

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Template file containing all content from previous templates, includes [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications#Standard R15 rigs|standard R15 rig]], body cages, attachment points.<br> <br> Use this template to rig and cage bodies and clothing.

[Download (.blend, .ma, .fbx)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/modeling/meshes/reference-files/Combined_Templates.zip)
