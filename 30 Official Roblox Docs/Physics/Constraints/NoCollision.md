*NoCollisionConstraint prevents collisions between two specific parts, but those parts may still register collisions with the rest of the world.*

The [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/NoCollisionConstraint|NoCollisionConstraint]] prevents collisions between two specific parts, but those parts may still register collisions with the rest of the world. Compared to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Workspace/Collisions#Collision groups|collision groups]], it provides a direct way to disable specific collisions, such as the wheel of a car scraping against the car's body.
