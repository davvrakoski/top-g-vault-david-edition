*The Mystery of Duvall Drive is a showcase demo with accompanying documentation on the concepts and processes used during development.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

[The Mystery of Duvall Drive](https://www.roblox.com/games/7902470429/The-Mystery-of-Duvall-Drive) is an official Roblox game used to showcase Studio's various features and tools to create an immersive environment. Returning from the [Kerr-Newman DSR-14 space station](https://www.roblox.com/games/7208091524/Beyond-the-Dark-Vistech-Showcase), we now invite you to explore the dark abandoned house and its many mysteries in this official demo, which is both uncopylocked and editable in Studio, as well as a great palette of assets and examples that you can model and use similarly in your own games!

Similar to the previous [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/Beyond the Dark/Beyond the Dark|Beyond the Dark]] showcase, this document demonstrates the process of going from simple sketches to a rich 3D environment with mind-bending visuals. Our goal is to provide information on both **how** we used Studio tools and **why** we used the features we did to achieve our results, including:

- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/MaterialService|MaterialService]]** and custom materials
- **Packages** and their ability to streamline processes
- **Volumetric audio** and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/SoundService|SoundService]]
- **Streaming enabled** for complex games

You can follow the articles sequentially or jump between the following sections:

- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Construct the house|Construct the house]]** explores the transformation from a simple blueprint to the final environment, including the process to ensure the environment was fun from start to finish.
- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Materialize the world|Materialize the world]]** details how both [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/MaterialService|MaterialService]] and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/SurfaceAppearance|SurfaceAppearance]] create the look and feel of each room's normal and corrupted state.
- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Immersive narrative|Immersive narrative]]** demonstrates how to utilize UI features to build lore throughout the playable area.
- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Stream in immersion|Stream in immersion]]** explains how we both code and constructed the game to take advantage of Streaming Enabled capabilities.
- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Develop a moving world|Develop a moving world]]** describes how and why movement can give the world a sense of life and reactiveness.
- **[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Resources/The Mystery of Duvall Drive/Design dark soundscapes|Design dark soundscapes]]** details how [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/SoundService|SoundService]] and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Sound/Sound objects#Volumetric|volumetric audio]] craft the distinct soundscapes of the normal and corrupted worlds.
