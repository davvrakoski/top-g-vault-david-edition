*Explains ways to continue learning about building in Studio.*

Congratulations on building your first obby with Roblox Studio! Now that you have experience creating and customizing basic parts with unique visuals and behavior, you can share your creation with others and extend your skills by learning about Studio's additional building features.

## Share with others

When you first publish a game, it's only playable by you as the creator. However, creating on Roblox is always more fun when you can share what you make with others. For steps on inviting early testers to preview your game, see [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Publishing/Create and publish games and places#Invite early testers|Invite early testers]]. For steps on how to release your game to players, see [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Publishing/Create and publish games and places#Release games|Release games]].

## Continue learning

Basic parts are an excellent start for building in the 3D space, but Studio offers many additional features to create environments in your games, including:

- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Parts/Materials|Materials]] that change the visual appearance and physical properties of parts.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Parts/Environmental terrain|Terrain Editor]] that generates realistic terrain environments, such as mountains, grass-covered hills, or a flat desert.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Parts/Textures and decals|Textures and decals]] that decorate parts with 2D images that either scale or stretch, respectively.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Parts/Solid modeling|Solid modeling operations]] that join parts together in unique ways to form more complex shapes known as unions or intersections.
