*Learn the basics of building games.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Using the [Classic Obby](https://www.roblox.com/games/203812057/Classic-Obby) template as a guide, this tutorial acts as an introduction to building in Roblox Studio using basic parts and AI Assistant. As you follow each chapter, you will learn how to create an obstacle course (obby) where players jump across platforms and avoid obstacles until they reach the final platform.

This lesson is intended for readers who are **brand new** to building with parts and working in Studio. If you are familiar with both, try another beginner lesson plan or jump straight to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Core/Core curriculum|building your first game]].

> [!info]
> If you don't have a Roblox account, Studio active on your device, or familiarity with Studio's UI, see the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Studio/Learn about Studio|Studio lesson]].

## Course contents

   > [!note]- Chapter 1 - Get started
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Building/Get started|open and customize]] the template's workspace so that you can focus on building your obby.

   > [!note]- Chapter 2 - Work with parts
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Building/Work with parts|create and design platforms]] for your obby using basic part shapes.

   > [!note]- Chapter 3 - Code with AI Assistant
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Building/AI Assistant|modify part behavior]] with code from AI Assistant.

   > [!note]- Chapter 4 - Test and save
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Building/Test and save|playtest your obby]] and publish your work to Roblox.

   > [!note]- Chapter 5 - Next steps
   > Learn where you can [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Building/Next steps|find more tutorials]] about building games with parts and meshes.
