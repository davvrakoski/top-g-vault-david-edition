*Learn the basics of creating special effects for games.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Using the [Mansion of Wonder](https://www.roblox.com/games/6901029464/Mansion-of-Wonder) sample as a guide, this tutorial acts as an introduction to creating special effects (VFX) in Roblox Studio using particle emitters. As you follow each chapter, you will learn how to create a laser blast and explosion that players can use as they defeat sinister slimes and ghastly ghosts in a spooky carnival game.

This lesson is intended for readers who are **brand new** to creating VFX art and working in Studio. If you are familiar with both, try another beginner lesson plan or jump straight to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Core/Core curriculum|building your first game]].

> [!info]
> If you don't have a Roblox account, Studio active on your device, or familiarity with Studio's UI, see the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Studio/Learn about Studio|Studio lesson]].

## Course contents

   > [!note]- Chapter 1 - Get started
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Artist/Get started|open and customize]] the template's workspace so that you can focus on creating colorful VFX.

   > [!note]- Chapter 2 - Work with particle emitters
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Artist/Work with particle emitters|change the appearance and behavior]] of particles in the carnival ride.

   > [!note]- Chapter 3 - Test and save
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Artist/Test and save|playtest your art]] and publish your work to Roblox.

   > [!note]- Chapter 4 - Next steps
   > Learn where you can [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Artist/Next steps|find more tutorials]] about VFX for games.
