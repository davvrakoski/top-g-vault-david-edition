*Learn the basics of coding for games.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Using the [Story Games](https://www.roblox.com/games/2546610365/Roblox-Creator-Challenge) sample as a guide, this tutorial acts as an introduction to coding in Roblox Studio using the Luau programming language. As you follow each chapter, you will learn how to create a story game where players can replace placeholder words with their own answers.

This lesson is intended for readers who are **brand new** to coding concepts and working in Studio. If you are familiar with both, try another beginner lesson plan or jump straight to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Core/Core curriculum|building your first game]].

> [!info]
> If you don't have a Roblox account, Studio active on your device, or familiarity with Studio's UI, see the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Studio/Learn about Studio|Studio lesson]].

## Course contents

   > [!note]- Chapter 1 - Get started
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Coding/Get started|open and customize]] the template's workspace so that you can work with your main script.

   > [!note]- Chapter 2 - Work with variables
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Coding/Work with variables|use variables]] to ask players questions and use their answers within your story.

   > [!note]- Chapter 3 - Test and save
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Coding/Test and save|playtest your story]] and publish your work to Roblox.

   > [!note]- Chapter 4 - Customize strings
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Coding/Customize strings|style your code]] with extra punctuation and line breaks.

   > [!note]- Chapter 5 - Next steps
   > Learn where you can [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Coding/Next steps|find more tutorials]] about coding for games.
