*Learn how to build 3D environments that enhance your game's gameplay.*

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwShvDmFHWc)

**Environmental art** is the discipline of creating and configuring 3D environments
that embody and facilitate gameplay requirements, immerse users within your game,
and provide contextual information about the world itself.

You'll learn how to recreate a [high-quality environment](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447845297/Environment-Art-Optimizing)
for a first-person shooter laser tag game by following step-by-step processes and
best practices for level design, developing assets, and optimization efforts for memory
and performance that you can then implement in your own games.

This course is intended for readers who are familiar
with general building concepts and tools in Roblox Studio. If you need help
learning how to build an environment, try the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Core/Core curriculum|core curriculum]].

## Course contents

   > [!note]- Chapter 1 - Greybox your environment
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Environmental Art/Greybox your environment|add simple shapes to the 3D space]] in order to figure out how users will
   > experience gameplay before investing time into polishing assets.
   >
   > [**Get the Place File**](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447721254/Environment-Art-Greyboxing)

   > [!note]- Chapter 2 - Develop polished assets
   > Learn about the concepts behind [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Environmental Art/Develop polished assets|designing high-quality assets]] in third-party modeling tools that replace the greybox environment.
   >
   > [**Get the Asset Library**](https://www.roblox.com/library/14447738661/Environment-Art-Asset-Library)

   > [!note]- Chapter 3 - Assemble an asset library
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Environmental Art/Assemble an asset library|import and configure your assets]] in a central location within your game for easy access and reuse.
   >
   > [**Get the Place File**](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447787049/Environment-Art-Assembling)

   > [!note]- Chapter 4 - Construct your world
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Environmental Art/Construct your world|utilize your asset library]], as well as how to add and configure additional elements of the 3D space to bring your world to life.
   >
   > [**Get the Place File**](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447826396/Environment-Art-Constructing)

   > [!note]- Chapter 5 - Optimize your game
   > Learn different strategies to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Environmental Art/Optimize your game|keep your frame rate and performance levels high]] across devices, especially those with memory limitations.
   >
   > [**Get the Place File**](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447845297/Environment-Art-Optimizing)
