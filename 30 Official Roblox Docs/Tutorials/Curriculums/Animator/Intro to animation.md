*Learn the basics of animating characters.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

Using the [Island of Move](https://www.roblox.com/games/5306359293/Island-of-Move) sample as a guide, this tutorial acts as an introduction to creating animations in Roblox Studio. As you follow each chapter, you will learn how to create keyframes for various poses, then loop them into a swimming animation that you can reuse in any game.

This lesson is intended for readers who are **brand new** to creating animations and working in Studio. If you are familiar with both, try another beginner lesson plan or jump straight to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Core/Core curriculum|building your first game]].

> [!info]
> If you don't have a Roblox account, Studio active on your device, or familiarity with Studio's UI, see the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Studio/Learn about Studio|Studio lesson]].

## Course contents

   > [!note]- Chapter 1 - Get started
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Animator/Get started|open and customize]] the template's workspace so that you can work with a poseable figure.

   > [!note]- Chapter 2 - Work with the Animation Editor
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Animator/Work with the Animation Editor|use the Animation Editor]] to create keyframes for different poses of an animation.

   > [!note]- Chapter 3 - Play your animation
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Animator/Play your animation|export and replace]] the character's default run animation with your new animation.

   > [!note]- Chapter 4 - Test and save
   > Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Animator/Test and save|playtest your animation]] and publish your work to Roblox.

   > [!note]- Chapter 5 - Next steps
   > Learn where you can [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Animator/Next steps|find more tutorials]] about animating characters for games.
