*Learn how to implement and organize a complex scripting project.*

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7iJKUUiKc0Y)

**Gameplay scripting** is the discipline of programming the behavior that makes games
engaging and fun to play, such as their logic, movement, events, and interactions between
objects in the 3D space.

You'll learn the gameplay scripting for a first-person shooter laser tag game by
following the general organization and key implementation details of a large, complex
project, including several opportunities to create new behavior with custom values.

This course is intended for readers who are familiar with general coding concepts, Studio's
data model, and the client-server relationship. If you need help learning how to code, try [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Fundamentals/Coding 1/Coding fundamentals|coding fundamentals]].

## Course Contents

> [!note]- Chapter 1 - Create teams
> Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Gameplay Scripting/Create teams|sort players into teams]] when a round is active.

> [!note]- Chapter 2 - Spawn and respawn
> Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Gameplay Scripting/Spawn and respawn|trigger unique actions]] when players spawn and respawn back into a round.

> [!note]- Chapter 3 - Add rounds
> Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Gameplay Scripting/Add rounds|implement rounds]] that track points, display final results, and reset teams.

> [!note]- Chapter 4 - Implement blasters
> Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Gameplay Scripting/Implement blaster behavior|script blast behavior]] for two different blaster types.

> [!note]- Chapter 5 - Detect hits
> Learn how to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Tutorials/Curriculums/Gameplay Scripting/Detect hits|perform hit detection]] from blast data, and allocate damage to reduce player health.
