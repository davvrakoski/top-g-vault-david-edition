*Used by [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/UITableLayout#MajorAxis|UITableLayout.MajorAxis]] to decide whether direct siblings are rows or columns.*

## Description

Used to indicate whether the direct siblings of a [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/UITableLayout|UITableLayout]] are considered the rows or the columns. The children of the direct siblings are the columns or rows, respectively.

## Items

| Name | Value | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RowMajor` | 0 | The direct siblings are considered rows. |
| `ColumnMajor` | 1 | The direct siblings are considered columns. |
