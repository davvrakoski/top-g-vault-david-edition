## Description

The WaterForce Enum is used to work with [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/Terrain|Terrain]] water cells.

## Items

| Name | Value | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `None` | 0 | No flow force on a legacy voxel terrain water cell; the water is still. |
| `Small` | 1 | A low flow force level of a legacy voxel terrain water cell. |
| `Medium` | 2 | A moderate flow force level of a legacy voxel terrain water cell, between [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Enums/WaterForce\|Enum.WaterForce.Small]] and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Enums/WaterForce\|Enum.WaterForce.Strong]]. |
| `Strong` | 3 | A high flow force level of a legacy voxel terrain water cell, one step below [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Enums/WaterForce\|Enum.WaterForce.Max]]. |
| `Max` | 4 | The maximum flow force level of a legacy voxel terrain water cell. |
