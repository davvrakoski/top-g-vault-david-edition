*Enum used by [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/Lighting#LightingStyle|Lighting.LightingStyle]] to indicate the artistic intent behind lighting in the experience.*

## Description

This enum indicates the artistic intent behind lighting in the experience. It is used by the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/Lighting#LightingStyle|Lighting.LightingStyle]] property.

## Items

| Name | Value | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Realistic` | 0 | The most advanced and realistic lighting and shadows Roblox can deliver. |
| `Soft` | 1 | A flat, retro-Roblox look with softer lights and shadows. |
