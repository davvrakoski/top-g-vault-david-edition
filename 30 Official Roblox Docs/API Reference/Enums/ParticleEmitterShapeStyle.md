*Controls whether a [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/ParticleEmitter|ParticleEmitter]] emits particles from the surface or the volume of its shape.*

## Description

Controls whether a [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/ParticleEmitter|ParticleEmitter]] emits particles from the surface or throughout the volume of the shape defined by [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/ParticleEmitter#Shape|ParticleEmitter.Shape]]. Used with the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/ParticleEmitter#ShapeStyle|ParticleEmitter.ShapeStyle]] property, which defaults to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Enums/ParticleEmitterShapeStyle|Enum.ParticleEmitterShapeStyle.Volume]].

## Items

| Name | Value | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Volume` | 0 | Particles emit from throughout the interior volume of the emitter shape. |
| `Surface` | 1 | Particles emit only from the outer surface of the emitter shape. |
