*An overview of topics to consider once you finish creating your game.*

Once you have created your game, you may want to find an audience for it
and keep them engaged over time. You need to consider:

- **Publishing** to make it public for everyone to enjoy.

- **Promotion** to bring in new users.

- **Monetization** strategies for selling any products within it.

- **Localization** to help users around the world understand it.

- **Analytics** to monitor how it's performing.

- **Game design** to create and maintain an engaging player experience.

## Publishing

When a game is ready to play, you can [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Publishing/Create and publish games and places|publish]] your
game on any devices you choose. A game can be published **privately** (default) in order to test and save to the cloud, or **publicly** so that it can be discovered and played by other users.

## Monetization

Users can spend Robux in games for items such as new vehicles or
power-ups, and creators can also earn Robux from [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Engagement-based payouts|engagement-based payouts]], [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Private servers|private servers]], [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Paid access in Robux|paid access in Robux]], and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Paid access in local currency|paid access in local currency]]. There are two main methods of
[[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Monetization|monetizing]] within a game:

- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Developer Products|Developer Products]] - users can purchase
  multiple times. Great for in-game currency purchases.

- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Monetization/Passes|Passes]] - users can only purchase once.
  Great for VIP passes or permanent power-ups.

Both are sold for Robux at a price you choose. You can then trade Robux for real
world currency using the **Developer Exchange**.

## Localization

Roblox players come from around the world and speak a variety of languages. It's
important to [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Localization/Localization|localize]] the game so everyone can
understand and enjoy it.

Roblox automatically captures text from a game and stores it in a table
for translation in the game's **Localization** page, where all localization settings can be configured.

## Promotion

You can promote your game within the Roblox Ecosystem in multiple ways:

- Use the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Promotion/Ads Manager|Ads Manager]] to create ad campaigns using image, video, or portal ads within existing games.
- Roblox [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Promotion/Experience events and updates|experience events]] are time-based events that users can join and discover through an events detail page.
- You can use integrated features such as [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Promotion/Experience events and updates|update announcements]] and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Promotion/Experience notifications|experience notifications]] to promote your content to a wider audience and keep in touch with your existing user base.

## Analytics

Roblox offers a variety of [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Analytics/Analytics|analytics]] features to help you chart your game's growth, track user behavior and retention, and find
opportunities for optimization.
