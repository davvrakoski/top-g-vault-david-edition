*The Rig Generator tool lets you insert pre-built character models.*

The **Rig Generator** tool lets you quickly insert basic pre-built character rigs into your games. You can also insert a rig of your account's avatar character, including character customizations and accessories. The tool inserts character [[30 Official Roblox Docs/API Reference/Classes/Model|Models]] with the appropriate joints and humanoid structure to support [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Animation/Animation in Roblox|animation]], [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Rigid Accessories/Rigid accessories|accessories]], and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Characters/Character appearance|character customizations]].

1. From the toolbar's **Avatar** or **Home** tab, click **Character**.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*

1. From the popup, select from the available options. You can insert either a legacy **R6** rig consisting of 6 mesh objects, or an **R15** rig which consists of 15 mesh objects, a [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Avatar/Character Bodies/Character body specifications#Standard R15 rigs|standard R15 rig]], and a significant expansion upon the R6's limited motion range.

   > [!warning]
   > Note that you can only equip clothing and other layerable accessories to **R15** models.

1. Choose a body shape of either **Masculine** or **Feminine** and then click one of the style buttons. The rig displays both in the 3D viewport and in the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Studio/Explorer window|Explorer]] window under the name **Rig**.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*
