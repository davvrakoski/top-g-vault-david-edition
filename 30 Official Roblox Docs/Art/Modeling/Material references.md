*PBR texture examples and various texture map values.*

When combining different combinations of metalness and roughness values with your PBR textures, Roblox can render virtually every type of surface with a high level of realism. The following includes various material values for common materials and [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Art/Modeling/Material references#Clothing examples|clothing examples]] that you can use when creating your own PBR surfaces.
