*Use Blender's Texture Painting tools to apply a surface appearance to your clothing item.*

With a new image ready to apply textures to, use Blender's Texture Paint mode to quickly brush texture colors onto your mesh:

1. Navigate to **Texture Paint** mode.
1. In the viewport, select your t-shirt mesh, then open the sidebar tools to access brush settings.
1. Select a color, brush size, and falloff. You might need to adjust settings, depending on the modifications you intend to apply.
1. Draw on either the UV 2D map or the 3D mesh.
1. Save the `.png` of your texture map by selecting **Image** > **Save**.

   [Video: Texturing_04.mp4](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/raw/main/content/en-us/assets/art/accessories/creating/Texturing_04.mp4)
