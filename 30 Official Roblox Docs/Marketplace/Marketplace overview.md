*Understand how to create and sell items on the Marketplace, including policies, fees and commissions, and managing IP.*

*(image in the full Top G Vault)*

The **Marketplace** is where all Roblox users can find avatar items to purchase and equip to their global avatar character. In the Marketplace, you can find character bodies and heads, clothing, accessories, animations, and other cosmetic items.

Users can access the Marketplace [through the web](https://www.roblox.com/catalog) or within the Roblox App. Developers can also set up access to the Marketplace within their games by using the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Players/Avatar Editor Service|Avatar Editor Service]]. Developers receive a commission based on sales of Marketplace items within their game.

> [!warning]
> The Marketplace is not the same as the [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Production/Creator Store|Creator Store]]. The Creator Store provides assets for creators to use for the development of their games, such as models, images, and plugins.

## Sell on the Marketplace

Creators can sell 3D accessories, clothing, and character bodies on the Marketplace. To ensure that assets you create meet Roblox's technical and community standards, make sure to review the following important policies and guidelines:

- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Marketplace/Marketplace policy|Marketplace policy]] — covers the rules and policies for Marketplace creators and their assets.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Marketplace/Intellectual property for avatar items|Intellectual property]] — provides an overview of how intellectual property works on the Marketplace.
- [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Marketplace/Moderation|Moderation]] — describes the process in which assets are moderated, including instructions on filing appeals and submitting DMCA requests.

If you have an asset ready to sell, and both you and your asset meet Roblox's policies and guidelines, you can begin [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Marketplace/Publish to Marketplace|uploading and publishing]] your assets to the Marketplace. Depending on the type of item sold, various fees and commissions can apply. For more information, see [[30 Official Roblox Docs/Marketplace/Marketplace fees and commissions|Fees and Commissions]].
