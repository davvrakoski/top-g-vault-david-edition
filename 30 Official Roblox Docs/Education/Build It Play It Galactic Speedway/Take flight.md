*Learn the character movement and flight controls for the Roblox Build It Play It Galactic Speedway template.*

Before designing your own driftspeeder, try flying a test speeder that's already in the garage to get an idea of the environment.

1. Click **Play** to test.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*

1. In the game, walk up to the robot character under the spaceship sign and press <kbd>E</kbd> to open up a menu. On that screen, click **Race**. In a race, you'll fly through rings to make the fastest time.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*

   Below are controls for in-game.

   | Action | Control |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Move** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or arrow keys |
   | **Rotate** | Hold the right mouse button and look around. |
   | **Pan** | Hold the middle mouse button to drag your camera around. |

1. For now, there's only one driftspeeder in the garage to choose from. Select it and then click **Start**.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*

1. Wait for the countdown to finish. While inside, take a minute to get used to flying with the controls below. Since you can't use the mouse while flying, remember to **exit** the speeder by pressing <kbd>E</kbd>.

   Below are controls for the speeder.

   | Action | Control |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Move the Speeder** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or arrow keys |
   | **Boost Speed** | <kbd>Shift</kbd> |
   | **Exit the Speeder** | <kbd>E</kbd> |

1. Stop playing by pressing the red square or press <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>F5</kbd>.

   *(image in the full Top G Vault)*
