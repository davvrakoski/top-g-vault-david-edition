# UGC Money Math - Upload Fee, Publishing Advance and Revenue Share

Numbers below are from the Creator Hub fees page as mirrored on 2026-10-25. Price floors change; check the live pricing page before you plan a launch.

## Three costs, one of them refundable

| Cost | Amount | Refundable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Upload fee** | 80 Robux per submission (500 Robux if the item uses an emissive mask). Every update to a published item costs the upload fee again. | No, not even if moderation rejects it |
| **Publishing advance** | Depends on type (table below), paid when you first publish | Yes, recouped from Roblox's share of your sales |
| **Free Limited per-unit fee** | Market-based, per unit (the FAQ quoted 100 Robux per unit at the time of writing) | No; Roblox keeps it |

## Publishing advance by type (non-Limited / paid Limited)

| Type | Non-Limited | Paid Limited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic shirt, pants, t-shirt | 10 | n/a |
| Hat, Face accessory | 1,500 | 13,000 |
| Hair | 1,000 | 6,000 |
| Neck, Shoulder, Front, Back, Waist | 1,000 | 15,000 |
| Layered jacket, shirt, t-shirt, sweater, pants, dress or skirt, shorts, shoes | 600 | 5,000 |
| Body | 2,500 | 20,000 |
| Head | 1,500 | 10,000 |
| Emote | 1,500 | 10,000 |
| Makeup | 1,000 | n/a |

Emissive items pay **5x** the standard non-Limited advance (for example a hat goes to 7,500).

## How the advance comes back

Each sale, your normal 30% creator share is paid, and on top of that Roblox's 30% platform share is redirected to you as a **rebate** until the advance is fully repaid. Rebates are processed daily and skip the 30-day escrow. After that, Roblox keeps its share again.

## Revenue share

| Where it sells | Creator | Game owner | Roblox |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace (website or app) | 30% base, rising with price (below) | none | rest |
| Inside a game | 30% | 40% | 30% |

**Progressive share (Marketplace sales only):** price at 1x floor gives 30%, 1.3x gives 37%, 1.5x gives 41%, 2x gives 50%, 3x gives 62%, 4x gives 67%, 6x and above caps at 70%. In-game sales always pay the base 30%.

All commissions sit in a **30-day escrow**.

## Worked example (original numbers)

A layered jacket, advance 600, floor assumed at 100 Robux, listed at 200 (2x floor).

- Upload: 80 Robux spent, gone.
- Publish: 600 Robux advance.
- Marketplace sale at 2x floor: 50% share = 100 Robux per sale, plus rebate from Roblox's share until 600 is repaid.
- In-game sale at 200: you get 60 (30%), the game owner 80 (40%), plus rebate while the advance is outstanding.

Break-even on the 80 upload fee happens on the first one or two sales; the advance is the real risk, and it only matters if the item never sells.

## Pricing tips

- Set **Amount Above Price Floor** (required) and optionally **Do Not Price Below**. If floors move, your item moves with them unless the minimum holds it.
- Pricing at 2x floor doubles your Marketplace share from 30% to 50%; test whether it kills volume.
- If you also own a game, sell your own items there: you earn the creator and the game-owner share (see [[Selling Avatar Items Inside Your Game - Commissions, Homestore and Creation Tokens]]).
- Robux from reselling items you did not create is not DevEx-eligible.
