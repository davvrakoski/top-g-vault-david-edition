# UGC Publishing Requirements 2026 - Who Can Sell Avatar Items

"UGC" on Roblox means avatar items made by the community: 3D clothing, accessories, bodies, heads, emotes and makeup, plus classic 2D shirts and pants. The old application-only UGC Program (launched in 2019) has been replaced by open requirements; the Marketplace FAQ confirms the legacy application process was sunset.

## Requirements table (Marketplace policy, Creator requirements)

| To do this | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Upload content from Studio or the Creator Dashboard | Government ID verification (or a linked parent account that is ID-verified), plus the 80 Robux upload fee |
| Publish to the Marketplace | ID verification, **2-step verification**, a publishing advance (10 to 2,500 Robux for non-Limited items, depending on type), and the individual or group owner must have **Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200** |
| Keep items on sale | Keep the Plus or Premium subscription (individual or group owner) |

Notes:

- **Roblox Plus** is a newer subscription; the docs say Plus subscribers have the same Marketplace access as Premium (publish, sell, resell Limiteds).
- You can publish as an individual and as part of a group.
- Losing the subscription or verification can take items off sale.

## Volume limits

- No cap on uploads.
- Enabling items for sale: no limit for non-Limited items, bodies or heads. The publishing page's table says no limit for paid Limiteds and **50 per day for free Limiteds**. The older FAQ text still says one Limited per day. When pages disagree, trust the Creator Dashboard at publish time.

## What gets items removed

From the Marketplace policy, in short:

- Breaking Community Standards (no political, religious, gory, violent, drug, sexual, dating or wedding themed items, or items meant for that roleplay).
- Near-duplicates of existing items, including re-publishing your own item as a Limited.
- Wrong category on upload.
- Bodies that include accessories, clothing, tattoos or makeup baked in (sell those separately), or missing modesty layers.
- Using another person's IP; see the official IP guidance.

## Practical first-item path

1. ID-verify and turn on 2-step verification.
2. Subscribe to Plus or Premium on the account (or group owner) that will publish.
3. Make one item from the official templates and pass validation in Studio.
4. Upload (80 Robux, not refunded on rejection), wait for moderation.
5. Publish with a price at or a little above the floor; pay the refundable advance.
