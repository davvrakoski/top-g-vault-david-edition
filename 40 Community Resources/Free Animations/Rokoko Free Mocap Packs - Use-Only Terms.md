# Rokoko Free Mocap Packs - Use-Only Terms

> **What:** small themed mocap packs that **Rokoko** (mocap suit maker) gives away as marketing. Clean suit + glove captures, already on game-standard skeletons, so they retarget to R15 easily. **Not bundle-safe:** the pages allow use, not redistribution.

## Packs checked

Pack | Contents (per page) | Skeletons | Format | Captured with
---|---|---|---|---
16 free walk and run cycles | 16 locomotion cycles, finger motion included; performer Sam Lazarus | Mixamo skeleton | FBX, 30 fps | Smartsuit Pro II + Smartgloves
Magic-themed pack ("Wingardium Leviosa" zip) | Spell-casting motions | Mixamo, UE4/UE5, HumanIK | FBX, 30 fps | Smartsuit Pro II + Smartgloves
TV-themed pack | Reality-TV, courtroom and documentary-style acting (count not stated) | Mixamo, Unreal, HumanIK | FBX, 30 fps | Smartsuit Pro II + Smartgloves

## Terms, as written on the pages
- Use "in any animation, VFX, game, 3D art" project, from passion projects to commercial use.
- Nothing on the pages allows **redistribution or resale** of the files, so treat them as **use-only**.
- Download = a sign-up form (name, email, company size, main 3D tool) that opts you into marketing email; no separate account is mentioned.

## Roblox notes
- Grab the **Mixamo-skeleton** export and reuse the Mixamo map: [[R15 Joint Map for Retargeting]] + [[Mixamo to R15 - Blender Retarget Workflow]].
- The walk/run cycles are not guaranteed in-place; flatten hip travel before upload ([[Mocap Cleanup for Roblox - In-Place, Loops and Foot Slide]]).
- Finger data is lost on a standard R15 rig.
- Keep the zips in a private folder; do not commit them to public repos or the vault.
