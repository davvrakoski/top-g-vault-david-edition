# Quaternius Universal Animation Library - CC0 Use Notes

> **Vault status:** the owner excludes Quaternius from anything bundled ([[Asset Licenses and Credits]] lists it under "Checked and left out"). The source pages themselves say **CC0**. So: fine to *use* in your own Roblox game, never copy into the vault's asset library.

## The two packs

Pack | Free "Standard" tier | Paid tiers (itch.io) | Content | Updates noted on page
---|---|---|---|---
**UAL 1** (March 2025) | 45 animations | Pro $9.99+ (120+ clips, FBX/GLB), Source $14.99+ (adds .blend rig) | 8-direction locomotion, jog, sprint, crawl, swim, sit, death, combat, gun actions, emotes | v2.0 new rig naming; v2.1 fixed clips exported at 24 fps instead of 30; v3.0 root motion on all locomotion, synced 8-way steps (all start left foot)
**UAL 2** | 42 animations (chest open, climb, wave, harvest, knockback, sword dash, **zombie walk and scratch**...) | Source $14.99+ | Melee and armed combat, parkour, farming, fishing, zombie locomotion | v2.0 16/6/2026, v2.1 5/7/2026, v2.2 28/9/2026

Both: one "universal humanoid" rig, stated to be compatible with common rigs such as Mixamo; versions with and without root motion.

## License as written by Quaternius
- Pack pages: CC0, "Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects."
- FAQ: all models CC0, no attribution needed (credit appreciated). The FAQ does not discuss reselling.

## Using UAL on Roblox
1. Download the **root-motion-disabled** variant for loops; Roblox moves the character itself.
2. Retarget to R15 with the Mixamo-style map in [[R15 Joint Map for Retargeting]] (UAL's rig is Mixamo-compatible per the page; check names in Blender after the v2.0 rename).
3. Check frame rate is 30 fps (v2.1 fixed old 24 fps exports; older downloads may still be 24).
4. UAL 2's zombie walk and knockback clips are handy placeholders for horde games; replace with custom work later if you need a signature look.

## Why "CC0 but not bundled"?
CC0 legally allows redistribution, but the vault owner made a project rule to keep Quaternius out of bundles. Respect the rule; do not "fix" it in this note.
