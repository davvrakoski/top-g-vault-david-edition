# Free Animation Credits - Attribution Lines

> Copy-paste credit lines for an in-game **Credits** screen or game description. Even where credit is optional, crediting is good practice and documents where each clip came from. Pair this with a private `SOURCES.md` in your project listing which animation ID came from which source.

## Credit lines

---|---|---

Quaternius UAL 1 / 2 (CC0) | No (appreciated) | Animations from the Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (CC0).
Rokoko free packs | Not stated | Some motion capture courtesy of Rokoko free mocap packs.
Mixamo | Not required | Some animations from Adobe Mixamo.
Your own video capture | n/a | Animation by <your name>.

## Rules of thumb
- **CC BY:** name the creator, the license, link the license, and say you changed it ("retargeted", "edited"). Credit must be reasonably visible - a Credits button in the menu works.
- **Use-only sources** (Mixamo, Rokoko, CMU-derived): crediting does **not** make redistribution OK. Credit, but still never share the raw files.
- Keep the license page URL with each source file in your private project folder; licenses change.
