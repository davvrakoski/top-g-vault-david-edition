# Cascadeur Plans - What Free Covers

> **Why this note exists:** Cascadeur (physics-assisted keyframe animation with AI auto-posing) is often recommended on Reddit as "the free animation tool". Since 2024 that is only half true for Roblox games. Checked on the official plans page, 2026-10-25.

## Plans (official page)

Plan | Price | FBX/DAE export | Commercial use | Retargeting
---|---|---|---|---
**Free (Basic)** | $0 | **No** - .casc only | **No** - "Only non-commercial use" | No
**Indie** | $19/month or $8/month billed yearly ($96/yr) | Yes | Yes, if revenue/funding is **under $100k per year** | Not listed
**Pro** | $49/month or $33/month billed yearly ($396/yr) | Yes | Unlimited | Yes (humanoid)
**Teams** | $49/user/month or $33/user/month yearly, 2-6 seats | Yes | Unlimited | Yes
**Educational** | per FAQ | Yes | **No** | -

Cascadeur's FAQ notes yearly Indie/Pro licenses turn perpetual after the minimum one-year term.

## What this means for a Roblox dev
- **Free plan cannot get an animation into Roblox** (no FBX) and is non-commercial; a Roblox game that earns Robux/DevEx is commercial. Use it only to learn.
- **Indie** is the cheapest legal route for a small studio; it covers most Roblox teams (under $100k/yr).
- Cascadeur publishes its own Roblox tutorial (cascadeur.com/tutor/robloxanimation, Cascadeur Team, needs version 2025.2+): R15 and R6 characters come pre-rigged in Cascadeur, AutoPhysics is recommended, and a companion video covers rigging/exporting the R15 character.
- Free alternatives for the same job: Blender + the free Rokoko retargeter ([[Rokoko Blender Add-on - Free Retargeting]]) or the built-in Clip Editor ([[Default Animation Editor - Practical Tips]]).
