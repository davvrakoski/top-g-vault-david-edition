# Animation Sources Checked and Left Out

> Companion to [[Asset Licenses and Credits]] ("Checked and left out"). Everything here was looked at on 2026-10-25 and **rejected for game use or listing**. If a source here changes its license, re-check its own page before moving it.

## Paid or limited tools sometimes called "free"
- **Cascadeur Free:** official plans page says non-commercial only and **no FBX export** (CASC only). Indie (under $100k/yr revenue) or Pro is needed for a Roblox game. See [[Cascadeur Plans - What Free Covers]].
- **Moon Animator 2:** a paid plugin. "Free Moon Animator" downloads circulating on third-party sites are unofficial copies; do not use them. See [[Moon Animator 2 - Paid Plugin Verdict]].

## Redistribution or provenance problems
- **"Mixamo plugin" style tools** that hand out a built-in list of Mixamo clips (e.g. the 2023 DevForum "Mixamo plugin released" by orxginaldw): this looks like redistribution of raw Mixamo data, which Adobe's FAQ forbids. Download from mixamo.com yourself.
- **Ripped AAA game animations**: e.g. a DevForum post ("AAA animation retarget to Roblox") retargeting *The Last of Us Part II* animations to R15 as a "test". Extracted game files are someone else's copyright; never use or list them, even as a test.
- **Leaked game mocap** (any leak, any studio): never.
- **Re-uploads of other creators' Roblox animations** ("animation stealers / spoofers"): uploading an animation you did not make or license is not allowed, and is no longer needed now that owners can share animations properly ([[Animation Permissions and Sharing - 2025 Update]]).
