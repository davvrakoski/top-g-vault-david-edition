# itch.io Free VFX Packs - License Checked

itch.io's free VFX tag (https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-vfx) is where many "free VFX pack" links on Reddit and YouTube point. Each page has two places for a license: the **"Asset license" field** in *More information* and the **description text**. Read both. This cluster lists a pack only when they agree on CC0, CC BY 4.0 or MIT.

## Converting itch sprite sheets for Roblox

Most itch packs are made for Unity/Godot. They ship as **horizontal strips** or odd grids with frame counts like 12, 30 or 60. Roblox flipbooks need a square power-of-two image in a 2x2/4x4/8x8 or custom grid, so re-pack them first: [[Converting Sprite Sheets into Roblox Flipbooks]].
