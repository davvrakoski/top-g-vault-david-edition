# Free VFX and FX - Credits List

Copy-paste credit lines for every resource this cluster lists. CC0 and MIT don't *require* credit in your game (MIT does require keeping the license notice when you redistribute the code). Crediting anyway is good practice and makes audits easy. Log what you ship in [[Asset and License Log Template]].
