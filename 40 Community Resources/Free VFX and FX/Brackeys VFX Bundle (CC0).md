A one-download starter kit from **Brackeys** (the game-dev YouTube channel) on itch.io: particle textures, flipbooks and pre-drawn sprite sheets gathered from CC0 sources and re-released as **CC0**, with the page stating attribution is not required. ## Roblox use

- **Static textures** go straight into `ParticleEmitter.Texture`, `Beam.Texture` or `Trail.Texture`.
- **Flipbooks**: check that the sheet is square, power-of-two and a 2x2/4x4/8x8 grid before upload. Otherwise re-pack it ([[Converting Sprite Sheets into Roblox Flipbooks]]).
- **Pixel-art sprite sheets** (CodeManu) are usually wide strips, so re-pack them too. Particle textures are filtered smoothly in Roblox, so pixel art goes soft when a particle is large on screen; keep those effects small or upscale the sheet (nearest-neighbour) before upload.
