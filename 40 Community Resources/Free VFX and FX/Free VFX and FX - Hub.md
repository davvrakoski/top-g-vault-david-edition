# Free VFX and FX - Hub

Free-to-use VFX for Roblox: texture and flipbook packs, Studio plugins, open-source effect modules, free tools, and technique write-ups summarised from DevForum and RealTimeVFX community tutorials. Every listed resource had its license read on its **own page** on 2026-10-25. Reddit was used for discovery only (it blocks direct fetching), and nothing is listed on a Reddit "it's free" claim alone.

**Rule of thumb:** CC0 / MIT = ship and bundle. CC BY 4.0 = ship and bundle with credit. GPL tools and Creator Store items = use only. Anything unclear = left out.
