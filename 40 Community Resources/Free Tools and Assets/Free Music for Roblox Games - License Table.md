# Free Music for Roblox Games - License Table

Music is where most copyright takedowns on Roblox happen. Only use tracks whose license you have read on the composer's or library's own page.

| Source | What | License (own page, 2026-10-25) | Credit? | Bundle-safe? | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Roblox Creator Store music** | Tracks from Roblox and its music partners, already on the platform | Roblox Terms; usable in your Roblox games | No | **Use-only** (never re-upload or bundle) | Studio Toolbox > Audio |
| **Incompetech** (Kevin MacLeod) | Large library of game-friendly tracks | Free Creative Commons option that **requires credit** (its credit generator; creators' credit lines read CC BY 4.0); paid Standard License if you can't credit | **Yes** | **Use-only with credit** (don't bundle the MP3s) | https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/ |
| **Kenney Music Jingles** | Short stingers (win, lose, level up) | CC0 (Kenney) | No | **YES** (already in [[Asset Licenses and Credits]]) | https://kenney.nl/assets/category:Audio |
| **Freesound** music loops | Community loops | Per file: CC0 / CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-NC 4.0 | Depends | CC0 and CC BY 4.0 (with credit) only | https://freesound.org |
| **Pixabay music** | Stock tracks | Pixabay Content License: no credit, no standalone redistribution | No | **Use-only** | https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/ |
| **OpenGameArt** CC0 music | Game tracks | CC0 for the CC0-tagged ones | No | **YES** (CC0 only) | [[More Free Sources - OpenGameArt CC0]] |

## Incompetech credit line
Put this in your game description or credits screen, once per track. Copy the exact line Incompetech's credit tool gives you; the common form is:
`"<Track Title>" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`

## Don'ts
- No popular songs, "slowed + reverb" edits, anime OSTs or game soundtracks, even if a YouTube video says "no copyright".
- No AI "sound-alike" tracks that imitate a real song.
- **FreePD** is often recommended in old threads, but its site now says it is permanently closed; don't use copies from mirrors.
