# Free Terrain, Lighting and Sky Plugins

Free on 2026-10-25 (Roblox asset API). Start with Studio's own [[Terrain Editor]] (it now covers generate, import heightmap, paint and sea level); add these only for what it can't do.

## How builders use them
- **Part to Terrain** is the usual way to make clean terrain roads, cliffs and paths: lay out parts at the exact shape, convert, then smooth with the terrain brushes.
- **Tree Generator** makes many parts per tree. For a forest, generate one tree, convert it to a mesh (or use a CC0 mesh tree), then reuse it as a package. See [[Procedural Vegetation Scatter and Biome Rules]].
- **Atmos** saves time getting a mood, but read the sky textures it uses before you ship: a preset sky is the plugin author's asset, not yours to resell. For your own skies see [[HDRI to Roblox Skybox - Free Converter Workflow]].
- Another popular "Celestial Body Dragger" (Maximum_ADHD) was **not free/off sale** on 2026-10-25, so it is left out.
