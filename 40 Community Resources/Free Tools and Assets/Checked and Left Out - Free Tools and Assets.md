# Checked and Left Out - Free Tools and Assets

Things people often recommend that were **checked on 2026-10-25 and not listed** as free-to-use resources in this folder. Re-check before adding any of them later.

## Unclear or unverified license
| Resource | Why left out |
|---|---|
| MagicaVoxel (ephtracy) | Official site shows no license or commercial-use terms |
| cgbookcase textures | Called CC0 by third parties, but its own license page did not load; not verified on the source |
| Inkscape | License page returned 403; not verified this session (link it once verified) |
| bfxr (increpare) | No license file found in the repo; use ChipTone or jsfxr instead ([[Free SFX Generators - ChipTone and jsfxr]]) |
| "Brushtool" scatter plugin | Creator and official listing could not be confirmed; only low-quality blog mentions |
| Den_S Blender add-on (pastebin) | Listed for use, but no license stated, so never redistributed ([[Free Rigging and Animation Helper Plugins]]) |

## Per-item licenses (owner policy: not listed as a source)
| Resource | Why |
|---|---|
| Poly Pizza | Each model carries its own Creative Commons license and the ToS defers to it; owner policy excludes it unless an item's CC0 / CC BY is verified one by one |
| Sketchfab "downloadable" models | Per-model licenses, often non-commercial; same policy |
| Quaternius, Mixamo, CMU mocap | Excluded by owner policy; see [[Asset Licenses and Credits]] and [[Free Animation Sources - License Table]] |
| Synty, Unity Asset Store, Fab / Megascans, TurboSquid, CGTrader | Paid-store or platform licenses; never bundle |

## Not free (paid or off sale on 2026-10-25)
Moon Animator 2, Codify, Load Character Pro, DataStore Editor, uiDesign Plus, Maximum_ADHD's Celestial Body Dragger / Tool Grip Editor / Mesh Optimization Tools, the GeomTools fan bundle. Details: [[Paid or Off-Sale Plugins Often Called Free]].

## Closed or restrictive
| Resource | Why |
|---|---|
| FreePD | Site says it is permanently closed |
| ShareTextures | Listed as **use-only**: its custom "CC0" bans redistribution in collections/plugins ([[More CC0 Texture Sites - 3DTextures.me and TextureCan]]) |

## Discovery note
Reddit (r/robloxgamedev, r/ROBLOXStudio, r/blender) blocked direct fetches during this pass, so community picks were cross-checked through DevForum threads, and every license was read on the resource's own page or repo.
