# More CC0 Texture Sites - 3DTextures.me and TextureCan

PBR texture sites that are **not yet in the vault**. Poly Haven and ambientCG are already covered: see [[SurfaceAppearance PBR from Poly Haven Textures]] and [[More Free Sources - ambientCG]]. Licenses below were read on each site's own page on 2026-10-25.

| Site | Who | What | License (own page) | Bundle-safe? | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3DTextures.me** | Gendo (ErenKatsukagi) | Free PBR materials, many stylized/tiling | CC0: use for any purpose incl. commercial, may share and include in projects; only rule is don't claim authorship | **YES** (CC0) | https://3dtextures.me |
| **TextureCan** | TextureCan | 650+ free PBR textures | CC0 1.0: any purpose incl. commercial, no credit needed, may redistribute with your projects and 3D assets | **YES** (CC0) | https://www.texturecan.com |
| ShareTextures | Ozgen Karagol Arslan, M. Tolga Arslan | PBR textures and models | **Custom "CC0" with extra rules**: no redistribution on other sites, in plugins or in collections without written permission; no automated downloads or add-on hotlinking | **Use-only** | https://www.sharetextures.com |

## Notes
- **ShareTextures is the trap.** It says "CC0" on the front page, but its license page adds restrictions that CC0 doesn't have. Fine for textures inside your own game; **never** put its files into this vault or a texture pack.
- **TextureCan's** terms warn that if a texture shows a brand, logo or copyrighted graphic, avoiding infringement is on you. Skip textures with visible logos.
- Keep each downloaded texture's source URL and license in [[Asset and License Log Template]].
- For Roblox: use the color, normal (OpenGL), roughness and metalness maps; see [[PBR textures]].

## Not listed
- **cgbookcase**: widely described as CC0 by third parties, but its own license page could not be loaded on 2026-10-25, so it is left out until checked.
