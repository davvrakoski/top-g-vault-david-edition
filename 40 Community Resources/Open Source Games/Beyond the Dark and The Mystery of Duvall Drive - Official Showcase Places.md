# Beyond the Dark and The Mystery of Duvall Drive - Official Showcase Places

**Publisher:** Roblox · **Terms:** uncopylocked by the creator (Roblox) on Roblox; no separate license. The Beyond the Dark page calls the demo "non-copylocked and editable in Studio"; the Duvall Drive page says it is uncopylocked and editable too. Roblox's Terms apply.

| Place | What it is | Showcases |
|---|---|---|
| Beyond the Dark (Vistech Showcase) | Explore the Kerr-Newman DSR-14 space station | Modular architecture, custom skinned creature characters, layered clothing suits, sound design, holo-screen and parallax UI |
| The Mystery of Duvall Drive | Dark abandoned house whose rooms flip between a normal and a corrupted state (follow-up to Beyond the Dark) | MaterialService custom materials, Packages workflow, volumetric audio, StreamingEnabled for a dense world |

**Genre:** sci-fi exploration; narrative horror-mystery.

## What you can reuse
- Modular kits and lighting setups as a quality bar for your own environment art.
- Custom character and creature rigs (Beyond the Dark); normal/corrupted room materials, moving-world animation scripts and UI-driven lore (Duvall Drive).
- Sound and streaming setups to copy into your own place.

## How to use them
1. Open the place link, three-dot menu, **Edit in Studio**.
2. **File > Save to Roblox As** to keep your own copy.
3. Read the matching official docs in the vault: [[Beyond the Dark]] and [[The Mystery of Duvall Drive]].

## Caveats
- These are demos, not full game loops. Use them for parts and techniques, then build your own game around them.
- They are heavy (high-fidelity assets); profile on mobile before shipping any of it.
- Do not re-publish them as-is or present them as official.
