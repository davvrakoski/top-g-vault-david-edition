# dwmk RobloxGames - Five Released Place Files (MIT)

**Author:** dwmk · **License:** MIT · **GitHub:** about 4 stars, created and last pushed 6 Aug 2024

## What it is
The author's backup of five of their own published Roblox games, released as open source as ready `.rbxl` place files.

| Game | What it is | File |
|---|---|---|
| Broken. | PvP combat arena with map randomizer, round timer, shop, menu and NPCs | `BrokenPVPgame_20240713_01.rbxl` |
| Demon Hunting Paradise | Combat game inspired by popular Roblox fighting and RPG games | `DemonHunting_20240717_04.rbxl` |
| Bangla Battlegrounds | Unfinished abilities-based battlegrounds game | `BanglaBattlegrounds_20240706_01.rbxl` |
| Photon. | Abstract experimental game (started as horror) | `Photon_2.rbxl` |
| Save Her | Abstract patience game | `SaveHer.rbxl` |

**Genre:** PvP arena, battlegrounds/fighting, experimental.

## What you can reuse
- Round timer + map randomizer + shop for a quick PvP arena.
- A battlegrounds-style abilities setup to study.
- Any scripts, UI or models the author made, under MIT.

## How to run it
1. Download the `.rbxl` you want from the repo.
2. Open it in Studio (File > Open from File) and press Play.
3. Publish as a new experience only after the audit below.

## Caveats
- The files are snapshots from release time; the live versions may differ.
- The README lists no third-party credits. Place files often contain Toolbox models, audio or animations from other creators. Open the Explorer, check every model's origin, and replace anything you cannot trace. Animations and audio by ID may not play in your game if they are owned by someone else.
- "Inspired by" big titles means do not copy their names, characters or move sets.
