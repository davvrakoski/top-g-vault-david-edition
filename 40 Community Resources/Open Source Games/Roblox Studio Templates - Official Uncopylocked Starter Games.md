# Roblox Studio Templates - Official Uncopylocked Starter Games

**Publisher:** Roblox Corporation · **Terms:** uncopylocked by the creator (Roblox) on Roblox; no separate license. Roblox's templates page calls them uncopylocked games meant as the starting point of your project, and describes publishing a template "as your own game". Roblox's Terms of Use still apply.

## What it is
The set of starter places on Studio's start screen (and the Creator Hub templates page). Each one opens as an editable copy you can publish as a new experience. These are the safest "full game" bases on Roblox because Roblox made them for exactly this purpose.

## How to use one
1. Open Roblox Studio; on the start screen pick the template (or open its place link and choose **Edit in Studio** from the three-dot menu).
2. **File > Save to Roblox As** a new experience under your account or group.
3. Change the name, icon, thumbnails, map and game feel before you publish publicly.

## Caveats
- Thousands of games start from the same templates. A lightly edited Classic Obby or Laser Tag will look like a copy; change the art and core loop.
- "Uncopylocked" is permission to copy the place, not a trademark grant: do not present your game as an official Roblox game.
- Some templates (Laser Tag, Platformer) depend on scripts Roblox may update; note the date you copied them.
