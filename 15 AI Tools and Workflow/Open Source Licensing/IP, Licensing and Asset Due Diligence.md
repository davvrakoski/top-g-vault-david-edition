# IP, Licensing & Asset Due Diligence

A technically excellent game can still be unsafe to ship if the team cannot prove it has rights to the assets/code.

## Track every external asset
Source URL, creator, license, purchase proof, date, allowed uses, attribution requirement, modifications and project locations.

## Open source
A GitHub repository being public does not mean you can use it however you want. Read the license. No license can mean you do not have permission to copy/distribute the code.

## AI assets
AI generation does not erase trademark, copyright or platform-policy issues. Avoid prompting for direct copies of protected characters/logos and review generated results before shipping.

## Marketplace
Keep receipts/licenses and verify that an asset does not contain unexpected scripts or dependencies.

## Lab
Pick 20 third-party assets and prove the license/source for every one. Anything uncertain goes into a quarantine list until resolved.
