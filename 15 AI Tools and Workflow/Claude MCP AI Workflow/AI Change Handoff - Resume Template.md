# AI Change Handoff / Resume Template

```markdown
# Resume Note
Date:
Branch/commit:

## Goal

## Current state

## Files changed
- path — why

## Tests actually run
- command/test — result

## Performance observations

## Security/data observations

## Known failures

## Do not redo

## Next exact step

## Relevant screenshots/logs
```

A good handoff lets another AI or human continue without reading the entire old conversation.
