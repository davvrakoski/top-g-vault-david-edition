# Obsidian MOCs, Atomic Notes & Links

A Map of Content (MOC) is a human-readable hub connecting related notes. Atomic notes focus on one useful concept; larger playbooks combine them for real workflows.

This vault intentionally uses both: cluster hubs for navigation and detailed production notes for execution.

## Link rule
Add links where knowledge interacts in practice — combat to animation/networking, weather to lighting/VFX, monetization to economy/analytics — not just because two words are similar.
