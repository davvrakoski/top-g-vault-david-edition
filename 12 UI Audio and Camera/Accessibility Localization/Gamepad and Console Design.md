# Gamepad & Console Design

Controller support is more than mapping keyboard keys to buttons.

## Navigation
Every menu needs deterministic focus order, visible focus state, a way back, and no focus traps. Test nested modals, inventories, shops and settings using only a controller.

## Gameplay
Tune stick deadzones, acceleration and sensitivity independently from mouse. Aim assist, target magnetism or lock-on should be intentional and genre-appropriate, not a hidden fix for poor controls.

## Readability
Players may sit farther from a TV than from a monitor. Avoid tiny text and information that only works at desktop distance.

## Lab
Complete your game's first 10 minutes without touching mouse or keyboard. Any moment that forces you to reach for them is a bug.
