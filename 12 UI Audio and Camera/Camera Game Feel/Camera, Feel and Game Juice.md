# CAMERA, FEEL & GAME JUICE

=================================================

CAMERA IMPROVES EVERYTHING:
- FOV shift on sprint
- controlled recoil
- small impact shake
- landing impulse
- lock-on framing
- obstruction handling
- shoulder swap
- vehicle camera lag
- aim sensitivity settings

GAME JUICE:
- hit stop
- sound layering
- recoil
- animation exaggeration
- particles
- screen flash
- controller vibration
- UI animation
- damage reaction
- environmental response

Rule:
Juice amplifies good mechanics. It cannot replace good mechanics.
