# 12-Week Roblox Developer Curriculum

## Week 1 — Studio + Luau fundamentals
Build: interactable door, pickup, simple HUD.

## Week 2 — Vectors/CFrame/math
Build: aiming, raycast, moving platform, camera exercise.

## Week 3 — Client/server + remotes
Build: secure interact/purchase system with validation.

## Week 4 — Data
Build: versioned player save + settings.

## Week 5 — UI/UX/input
Build: inventory that works on desktop/mobile/gamepad.

## Week 6 — Animation/character feel
Build: polished movement + attack timing.

## Week 7 — AI/pathfinding
Build: patrol/investigate/chase/search enemy.

## Week 8 — Combat/VFX/audio
Build: one complete weapon with readable feedback.

## Week 9 — 3D art pipeline
Build: one Blender/Tripo-cleaned prop from reference to Roblox.

## Week 10 — Performance
Profile, create a bottleneck intentionally, fix it, record before/after.

## Week 11 — Analytics/discovery
Instrument onboarding funnel and make truthful thumbnail variants.

## Week 12 — Vertical slice
Ship a 5–10 minute polished slice and run a release checklist.
