# Dialogue, Voice, Subtitles & Ducking

Dialogue needs intelligibility before cinematic effects.

Use subtitles, speaker identification when needed, localization-ready text, and controlled music/SFX ducking. Spatial voice lines should remain understandable when they carry mission-critical information.

## Lab
Test dialogue during the loudest combat scene and on low-volume speakers.
