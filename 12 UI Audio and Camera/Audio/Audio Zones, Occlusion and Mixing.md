# Audio Zones, Occlusion & Mixing

Audio is one of the cheapest ways to make a world feel larger and more expensive.

## Mix hierarchy
Protect critical information: dialogue, danger cues and interaction sounds should not be buried under ambience or music.

## Zones
Define interior/exterior, underground, combat and special-space mix states. Transition smoothly instead of instantly replacing every sound.

## Occlusion
When appropriate, reduce or filter sounds blocked by solid geometry. Do not raycast hundreds of sound sources every frame; update only relevant nearby sources at a sensible cadence.

## Variation
Footsteps, impacts and weapon sounds need multiple samples or controlled pitch/volume variation to avoid machine-gun repetition.

## Lab
Mix one encounter at three distances: headphones, desktop speakers and TV-like volume. If an important cue disappears, fix hierarchy before adding more sounds.
