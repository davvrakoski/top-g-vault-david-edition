# Footsteps & Surface Audio

Footsteps should be driven by animation contact and material response, not a blind timer.

Select sound family from the surface, then vary sample/pitch/volume within controlled ranges. Sprint, crouch, landing and wet surfaces can use different layers.

## Lab
Run the same animation at multiple speeds and verify steps still happen at visual foot contacts.
