# AUDIO

=================================================

AUDIO CATEGORIES:
Music
UI
Weapons
Impacts
Footsteps
Ambience
Weather
NPC
Vehicles
Voice
Stingers

Use positional audio for world sources.
Use SoundGroups or modern audio routing/mixing to control categories.

FOOTSTEPS:
surface material
+ movement speed
+ animation marker
+ small random pitch/variant
= convincing footsteps.

INTERIOR AUDIO:
Use reverb/EQ/filters to create a different space than outdoors.

DO NOT:
Play the same exact sound at identical volume/pitch 40 times.
