# Dynamic Music & Combat States

Dynamic music can react to exploration, danger, combat, boss phases and victory.

## State model
Exploration -> Tension -> Combat -> Climax -> Resolution. Crossfade or layer stems smoothly and prevent rapid state flapping.

## Gameplay
Music should support danger cues, not cover them. Duck music under critical dialogue/UI alerts when appropriate.

## Lab
Play a full encounter with eyes closed for part of it; important state changes should still be understandable from audio.
