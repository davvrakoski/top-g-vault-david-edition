# In-Game Screenshot Staging for Marketing

Marketing images can be staged without becoming dishonest. Use real models, real environments and real gameplay-capable actions, then improve camera, lighting, pose and timing.

Do not show destruction, weapons, characters or mechanics that the released game does not contain.

## Lab
Create a shot list: hero close-up, combat action, environment discovery, social moment and progression/reward.
