# PROMPT LIBRARY FOR TRIPO / 3D GENERATION

=================================================

GENERAL PROP:
"Create a game-ready 3D [OBJECT] for Roblox.
Style: [STYLE].
Clean silhouette readable from gameplay distance.
Production-friendly topology with no unnecessary internal geometry.
Symmetrical where appropriate.
Separate moving pieces into logical objects.
Clean UV-ready surfaces.
No floating geometry, fused parts, text, logos, or watermarks.
Neutral lighting in preview.
Designed for cleanup, retopology, UV work, and export to Blender."

CHARACTER:
"Create a stylized Roblox-compatible humanoid character reference/model in a clean T-pose.
Keep hands/feet anatomically consistent with the requested Roblox style.
No random extra fingers.
Clear separation between clothing and body where requested.
Symmetrical limbs.
Neutral expression.
Clean silhouette.
No embedded background.
Model must be suitable for retopology, rigging, weight painting, Blender cleanup, and later Roblox import."

CLOTHING:
"Create ONLY the clothing item: [ITEM].
No body, skin, head, hands, feet, mannequin, or invisible human geometry.
The garment must be hollow and wearable.
Open neck/cuffs/waist/hem as appropriate.
Symmetrical.
Neutral pose matching a standard humanoid T-pose.
Clean seams and moderate folds.
No brand logos, copyrighted symbols, text, or random decals.
Designed for Blender fitting, retopology, UVs, and rigging."
