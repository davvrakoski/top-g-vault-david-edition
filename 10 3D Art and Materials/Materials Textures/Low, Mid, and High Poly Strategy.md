# Low, Mid, and High Poly Strategy

Use "poly level" as an asset-role decision, not a quality label.

High-detail source:
- sculpt/bake source
- hero reference

Game hero mesh:
- silhouette/deformation receive geometry
- microdetail moves to textures/normals

Repeated gameplay prop:
- efficient silhouette
- reused materials

Background:
- simple geometry
- aggressive distance reduction

A clean low-poly asset can look more professional than a noisy high-poly AI mesh.
