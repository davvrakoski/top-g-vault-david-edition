# Economy: Inflation, Sources & Sinks

An economy is a flow system.

## Sources
Quests, kills, daily rewards, sales, events and purchases create value.

## Sinks
Upgrades, crafting, cosmetics, repairs, rerolls, trading fees or prestige systems remove or transform value.

## Inflation
If sources continuously exceed sinks, prices stop meaning anything. Track currency created per player per day, currency spent, median balances and balance distribution — not only total revenue.

## Fairness
Avoid systems where a new player must compete directly with years of accumulated currency without catch-up design.

## Lab
Create a spreadsheet model for 30 days of play for casual, regular and hardcore players. Ask where each group runs out of goals or becomes impossibly rich.
