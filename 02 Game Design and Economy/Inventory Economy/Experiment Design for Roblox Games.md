# Experiment Design for Roblox Games

Good experiments change one hypothesis at a time.

Template:
**Problem:** New players leave before first reward.
**Hypothesis:** Reducing time-to-first-reward from 4 minutes to 90 seconds lowers first-play bounce.
**Variant:** New onboarding/reward placement.
**Primary metric:** onboarding completion / bounce.
**Guardrails:** session quality, error rate, economy inflation.
**Duration/sample:** decide before reading results.
**Decision:** ship / revert / iterate.

Do not keep tweaking the experiment while it is running unless there is a bug or safety issue; otherwise you no longer know what caused the result.
