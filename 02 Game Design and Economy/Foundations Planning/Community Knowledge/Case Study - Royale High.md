# Case Study - Royale High

Royale High (callmehbob) is a fantasy school roleplay and dress-up game: attend classes, earn Diamonds, buy outfits and accessories, decorate a dorm. It matters because it shows how deep cosmetic customization and community-made content (stories, halos, sets) can carry a game for nearly a decade without combat.

## Core loop
1. Attend timed classes or minigames; earn Diamonds and XP.
2. Buy clothing, accessories and dorm items from shops.
3. Customize: colors, fabrics, materials, stickers.
4. Show off in social spaces; enter seasonal events and contests.
The page's current update lists a new set, candy shop accessories, the returning "Royalloween" realm, a new halo, physics hair and new walk animations.

## Session shape
Medium sessions: a class or event loop of a few minutes, plus open-ended dressing and socializing.

## Progression and economy
- Diamonds are the soft currency; levels unlock perks (community wiki).
- Halos are the prestige items: seasonal, earned through events (fountain stories, per the page) and traded.
- Contests: the "Fountain Stories" with winners and honorable mentions reward player writing with badges and in-game recognition (page).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Eight passes, all creative tools: Hair Colors + Glowing (100, 4,500+ colors), Materials (100), Sticker Packs (125), Special Fabric Designs (150, 7,000+), Upload Custom Fabrics (200), Faster Flight (299), Paintbrush (300), Crystal Ball (300, PC only). Plus Diamond packs (Developer Products, in-game). The pattern: sell **more ways to express yourself**, not new items to grind.

## Social hooks
- Dress-up is for being seen; other players are the audience.
- Community writing contests turn fans into contributors and get their names in the game (badge holders shown in an audience).
- Collaborators credited by name in the description (a halo "by _Cheepi").

## Update cadence
Frequent seasonal content; the page shows a long "what's new" list and was updated the day before this snapshot.

## Why it grew
- Fantasy princess/school fantasy served an audience few games targeted in 2017.
- Very high cosmetic depth for its time (physics hair, animated items).
- Seasonal halos created long-term collectible value.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Sell creative tools.** Color, fabric, material and upload passes multiply every item you already made.
2. **Run player content contests.** Stories, outfits or builds with in-game credit make players feel like co-authors.
3. **Make one prestige item per season.** A seasonal halo is a reason to play every event.
4. **Credit collaborators on the page.** It builds trust and attracts more creators.
5. **Update patch notes in the description.** A long, fresh "NEW" list tells returning players what to try.
