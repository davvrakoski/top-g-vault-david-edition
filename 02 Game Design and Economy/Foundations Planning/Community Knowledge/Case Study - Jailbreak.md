# Case Study - Jailbreak

Jailbreak (Badimo: badcc and asimo3089) is an open-world cops-and-robbers game: criminals escape prison and rob stores, banks and trains; police hunt them. It matters because it is the reference design for asymmetric team open worlds on Roblox, and because its later years show what happens when exploiting outpaces content.

Released April 2017; earned over US$1 million in its first year (Wikipedia list). Made by a team of two at launch (Guinness record page for most likes). Co-creator Alex Balfanz paid for his Duke University education with game funds (Wikipedia). A film was announced in November 2025.

## Core loop
- **Criminal**: escape prison, get a car, rob a location, survive the escape timer, cash out at a hideout, buy faster cars and better gear.
- **Police**: patrol, chase, arrest criminals with handcuffs for bounty cash.
Both sides share one map and one economy (cash buys vehicles, customization, weapons).

## Session shape
Short robbery runs (minutes each) inside a long open session. Timed heists that open and close on a schedule create "it's open now" moments across the server (unverified detail of current schedule).

## Progression and economy
- Cash ladder of vehicles is the main progression: each car is faster and more desirable.
- Seasons with ranked rewards and limited vehicles (per community wiki history).
- Trading of limited vehicles and items via trade servers.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Seven passes: Car Stereo (200), Duffel Bag (345, top tier bag, carry more loot), VIP Trading (350, trade-server features), Crime Boss (400) and SWAT Team (400) side perks, Pro Garage (450, spawn your cars anywhere), VIP Lifetime (750). Note the matched pair: one pass per side.

## Social hooks
- The two teams create rivalry and stories in every server.
- Vehicles are status symbols chased by both sides.
- Trading servers turn collectors into a community.

## Update cadence
Once a famous weekly-ish update game; the page now says the team is focused on cheating before rolling out remaining updates. Updated 2026-10-07.

## Why it grew
- A more fleshed-out version of the Prison Life idea (Wikipedia list), with polished vehicles and robberies.
- Two-person team with complementary skills (programming plus design/building, per Wikipedia and Guinness).
- Strong early YouTube coverage and a Roblox event feature (Ready Player One).

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Asymmetric teams in one world.** Cops vs robbers gives two loops from one map and keeps PvP meaningful.
2. **Sell one perk per side.** Crime Boss and SWAT at the same price feel balanced and fair.
3. **Vehicles as the ladder.** A visible car you drive is a better progression reward than a stat.
4. **Budget for anti-exploit early.** A late-life game whose page says "focused on cheating" is a warning: server-side checks must come before content scale.
5. **Small team, clear split.** One builder-designer and one programmer can ship a genre-defining game.
