# Case Study - Pet Simulator 99

Pet Simulator 99 (BIG Games Pets) is the third big entry in the Pet Simulator series: hatch pets from eggs, send them to break coins and chests, unlock new zones, repeat. It matters because it is the most refined example of the "egg simulator" money engine on Roblox, and its store page shows exactly how a luck-and-automation pass ladder is built.

Released 2023-12-01; select Pet Simulator X pets could be transferred at launch (Wikipedia list). Its predecessor won the 2023 Innovation Award for Most Concurrent Users with a 1.48M peak. Entertainment Focus praised the fortnightly update schedule (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. Your pets auto-break "breakables" (coins, chests) in the current zone.
2. Coins buy eggs; eggs hatch random pets of rising rarity.
3. Stronger pets break things faster; buy the next zone gate.
4. Ranks and quests add goals; rebirths reset for multipliers (per series pattern).

## Session shape
Very idle-friendly: you can play actively (tap, open eggs) or leave pets farming. Many short "open 15 eggs" moments.

## Progression and economy
- Zone gates are the spine; each zone has better eggs.
- Rarity layers on top of rarity: Golden, Rainbow, Shiny and Huge versions of pets.
- Trading and booths add a player market; Huge pets are the status items.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
13 passes that map almost exactly onto the loop's slow points:
- Automation: Auto Farm (175), Auto Tap (350).
- Capacity: +15 Pets (375), +15 Eggs per open (625), Daycare Slots (625).
- Luck: Lucky +200% (275), Ultra Lucky +500% (800), Magic Eggs (1,200), Super Shiny Hunter (1,600), Huge Hunter (3,250).
- Rewards: Super Drops (2,400), Double Stars (2,400).
- VIP (400): VIP mine, hoverboard, booth.
Several luck passes say "PERMANENT, STACKS". Seasonal limited packs sell through Developer Products (third-party trackers).

## Social hooks
- Trading plaza and booths; Huge pets as flex items.
- Server-wide boosts and events make players care who else is online (unverified detail).
- Toys (PhatMojo, McDonald's Happy Meal in Nov 2024) push the brand beyond Roblox.

## Update cadence
Frequent: the fortnightly schedule noted by press; the page title rotates with each update ("[PSX]" now).

## Why it grew
- Brand carried over from Pet Simulator X's 1.48M CCU audience.
- Pet transfer from the previous game rewarded loyal players for moving.
- Creator Preston's own large YouTube audience (BIG Games founder) gives built-in promotion (unverified as a measured cause).

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Map each pass to a bottleneck.** Time (auto), space (slots), and odds (luck) are the three things grinders pay to relieve.
2. **Layer rarity variants.** Golden/Rainbow/Shiny/Huge multiply the collection without new models.
3. **Let sequels inherit.** Transferring a few items to the new game keeps the old audience.
4. **Fixed update rhythm.** A two-week cadence is a promise players plan around.
5. **Watch the ethics.** Stacking luck passes on paid eggs is close to gambling for kids; follow Roblox's paid random item rules and show odds.
