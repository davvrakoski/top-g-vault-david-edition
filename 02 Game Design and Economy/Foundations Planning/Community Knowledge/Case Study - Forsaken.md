# Case Study - Forsaken

Forsaken (Forsaken Dev Team) is an asymmetric horror game in the Dead by Daylight mold: several Survivors complete objectives and survive until a timer hits zero while one Killer hunts them. Its characters are drawn from Roblox culture and myths. It matters because it shows the 1-vs-all format thriving on Roblox with a character roster, per-character progression and a small, cosmetic-only pass store, while still labelled beta.

## Core loop
From the page:
1. Survivors protect teammates, complete objectives (generators in guides) and survive until the timer hits 0.
2. The Killer must eliminate everyone.
3. Roles rotate between rounds (selection details on the community wiki).
4. Earn EXP per character; unlock and level characters and skins.

## Session shape
Short rounds (minutes) in small servers (9): one Killer plus eight Survivors. Intensity is high; the page warns about flashing lights and loud audio.

## Progression and economy
- Character roster with unique abilities for both sides; mastery per character.
- EXP per character is the main grind (the VIP pass boosts it).
- Skins by theme, released in packs.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Four passes: Emote Pack #3 (189), 2x Emotes slots (199), Skin Pack #4 (349, five skins), V.I.P (599, 25% more EXP for all characters). Character and currency purchases happen in-game (unverified detail). Numbered packs (#3, #4) suggest a steady release of small cosmetic drops.

## Social hooks
- Asymmetric fear: one player is the monster, others coordinate.
- Characters based on Roblox lore and community figures make the game feel "of the platform" and drive fan art and theories (unverified as a measured cause).
- Emotes credited to named community creators on the pass.

## Update cadence
Very active (updated on the day of this snapshot); weekly themed beats appear in tracker title history ("WEEK 2", "WEEK 3", unverified).

## Why it grew
- Dead by Daylight-style play adapted to short Roblox rounds and mobile.
- Roblox-native characters and lore give an identity no outside game has.
- Awards and creator coverage in 2025.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Asymmetric 1-vs-many with a timer.** A survive-the-clock goal is simpler than escape-gate logic and keeps rounds short.
2. **Per-character EXP.** Levels per character make every unlock its own progression track.
3. **Numbered cosmetic packs.** Small themed packs released regularly are easy to produce and to market.
4. **Label beta honestly.** A visible "beta" tag sets expectations while you balance.
5. **Credit community creators.** Naming contributors on items invites more contributions.
