# Case Study - DOORS

DOORS (LSPLASH) is a co-op roguelite horror game: open numbered doors through a procedurally arranged hotel while "entities" try to kill you, each with its own rule you must learn. It matters because it shows how to make horror replayable: learnable monster rules, short deaths, and a group that watches each other scream.

Released 2022-08-10 with one floor (The Hotel); hotel revamp 2023-01-29; modifiers 2023-08-26; second floor The Mines 2024-08-30; admin panel and stream integration 2024-12-20 (Wikipedia list). First teaser trailer posted 2022-07-16 (Know Your Meme).

## Core loop
1. Form a small party in the lobby, enter the elevator.
2. Move door to door; loot drawers for gold, keys, lighters and items.
3. Survive entities, each with a tell and a counter (hide in a closet, stop moving, look away; details on the community wiki).
4. Reach floor milestones; earn Knobs (meta currency) and achievements.
5. Die, learn the rule, retry.

## Session shape
A full run takes a long sitting, but deaths come early and often, and a retry is one elevator ride away. The page itself says: enter without a guide and use each death as a lesson.

## Progression and economy
- Knobs buy pre-run boosts and revives.
- Achievements and a death counter are shown to other players, so mastery is visible.
- Modifiers add hard mode for experts after they beat the game.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Three passes: Halloween '26 Buster Pass (99, a second reward row on the event pass), Kitty Bundle (299, flashlight skins), Admin Panel (499, spawn entities and items in your server). Revives and Knobs are also sold. The page offers a free Revive for joining the LSPLASH group and Knobs plus a Revive for buying and wearing their UGC.

## Social hooks
- Co-op horror is reaction content; PCGamesN described it as too much for YouTubers to handle.
- Admin Panel and stream integration let creators troll their friends or chat, which creates more videos.
- Group-join rewards turn players into followers you can notify.

## Update cadence
Large content drops months apart (floors), plus seasonal events and a Hunt participation (The Backdoor sub-floor).

## Why it grew
- Built on the style of an earlier Roblox game, Rooms, with much higher production value (Wikipedia list).
- Pre-launch trailer seeded hype; creators drove the August 2022 surge (PCGamesN).
- Every entity is a meme-able character, which spread on video platforms.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Give each monster one learnable rule.** Players feel smarter on each run; deaths feel fair.
2. **Make dying fast and cheap.** Short failure plus instant retry keeps a horror loop from feeling punishing.
3. **Sell sandbox tools to creators.** An admin panel is a pass that also manufactures marketing.
4. **Reward group joins and UGC wear.** Free revives for joining build your notification audience.
5. **Ship a trailer before launch.** DOORS posted a teaser almost a month before release.
