# Case Study - Fisch

Fisch (Fisching) is a fishing adventure: cast, play a short reeling minigame, catch fish with huge variety, sell or appraise them, buy better rods and explore new islands. It matters because it took a cozy, ancient genre and gave it a skill minigame, massive variant counts and an explorable world, a good template for any "catch and collect" loop.

## Core loop
From the page's how-to:
1. Hold to cast the bobber; shake the rod to attract fish faster.
2. On a bite, hold/click to keep a white bar on a moving blue line.
3. Fill the progress bar to land the catch (mistakes allowed).
4. Sell fish for money (C$), or appraise and enchant for value.
5. Buy better rods and boats; travel to new islands with rarer fish.

## Session shape
Each catch is under a minute; sessions are relaxed and long, often social (fishing next to others).

## Progression and economy
- Rods are the main gear ladder; enchantments add modifiers.
- Fish have rarity, size and mutation variants (that is where the 400k number comes from).
- A bestiary/journal and quests give completion goals (community wiki).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Nine passes, mostly convenience "anywhere" perks:
- Emote Pack (99), Bobber Pack (99), Supporter (169, tag and halo, 1000 C$).
- Enchant Anywhere (239), Double XP (239), Spawn Boat Anywhere (299), Appraisers Luck (299), Sell Anywhere (399), Appraise Anywhere (499).
The "Anywhere" family removes travel back to town: the loop's main friction.

## Social hooks
- Shared spots and rare catches announced to the server (community wiki detail, unverified).
- Weekly or event fish create common goals.

## Update cadence
Frequent themed updates: the page title tag changes each update ("[HORSEMAN]" now; trackers show many past tags like Astral, Racing, Journal). Updated the day before this snapshot.

## Why it grew
- Fishing is universally understood and relaxing.
- The reeling minigame adds skill, so it is not pure idle.
- Huge variant space means "one more cast" can always be the rare one.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Add a skill minigame to a collection loop.** A short, forgiving bar game makes each catch feel earned.
2. **Generate variants from axes.** Species x size x mutation x rarity creates enormous counts with few models.
3. **Sell removal of travel friction.** "Do X anywhere" passes target the most annoying part of the loop without touching odds.
4. **Tag your title with the update.** A bracket tag in the name tells returning players something new is live.
5. **Explain controls in the description.** Fisch's page is a mini tutorial.
