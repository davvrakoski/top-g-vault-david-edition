# Case Study - Rivals

Rivals (Nosniy Games) is an arena FPS built around duels: step on a duel pad, fight 1v1 up to 5v5, first to 5 round wins takes the match. It matters because it became the dominant Roblox shooter of 2024-2026 by making matchmaking physical (stand on a pad) and building a key-and-crate economy around fair, unlockable weapons.

## Core loop
1. Spawn in a social lobby; step on a duel pad to challenge someone (or queue a team mode).
2. Pick your loadout (primary, secondary, melee, utility).
3. Fight short rounds in a small arena; movement (dash, slide, bounce) matters.
4. First to 5 wins; earn keys and XP.
5. Spend keys on crates for new weapons and skins; complete contracts for exclusive rewards.

## Session shape
Rounds are seconds to a minute; a match is a few minutes. The lobby itself is the queue, so there is no menu wait.

## Progression and economy
- Keys unlock weapons; skins and wraps come from crates.
- Contracts (missions) give goals; win streaks and leaderboards give status (page).
- Weapons are unlockable for everyone, so the Robux store mostly sells faster access plus exclusive skins.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Eight bundles, each "weapon + exclusive skin + extras": Starter Bundle (59, crate + Shorty + skin), RPG Bundle (95), Medkit Bundle (289), Exogun Bundle (649), Standard Weapons Bundle (849, every current and future standard weapon, with key refunds), Classic Bundle (865), Energy Bundle (975), Heavy Duty Bundle (1,425). The 59 Robux starter is a classic first-purchase offer.

## Social hooks
- Duel pads make challenges public: everyone sees who called out whom.
- Win streaks shown in the lobby.
- Group join rewards (page).
- Strict anti-cheat message on the page: permanent bans, no second chances.

## Update cadence
Numbered updates (Update 24 for Halloween on the page) every few weeks (exact rhythm unverified).

## Why it grew
- Fast, readable fights on every platform including console and mobile.
- Physical matchmaking is a social, low-friction innovation.
- Fair unlocks plus cosmetic chase; strong anti-cheat stance in a genre plagued by exploits.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Make matchmaking a place.** Duel pads in a lobby turn queueing into a social action.
2. **Short rounds, first to N.** First-to-5 makes comebacks possible and matches short.
3. **Offer a tiny starter bundle.** A sub-100 Robux first purchase converts players who have never paid.
4. **"All current and future" bundles.** Selling future content up front rewards your biggest fans.
5. **Publish your anti-cheat stance.** Competitive players stay where they trust results.
