# Case Study - Natural Disaster Survival

Natural Disaster Survival (Stickmasterluke) is the classic round survival game: a random map appears on an island, a random disaster hits (earthquake, tornado, tsunami, fire, flood and more), and everyone alive at the end wins. It matters because it is the minimum viable round game: one rule, physics destruction and randomness, still played by thousands after nearly two decades.

PCGamesN listed it among the best Roblox games; a University of Auckland academic criticised some of its implied real-world disaster advice (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. Lobby wait; the next map spawns.
2. A disaster is announced and starts.
3. Survive by running, climbing, hiding; buildings break with physics.
4. Survivors win the round and are listed; next round.

## Session shape
Rounds of a couple of minutes; drop in, drop out at any time. No progression is needed to enjoy a round.

## Progression and economy
Almost none: survival counts and wins act as score. The game sells convenience items rather than progress.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Three cheap passes, each a spawn item: Yellow Compass (60), Red Apple (80), Green Balloon (95, lets you float down from heights). The balloon is a classic "fun advantage": it helps in some disasters but never guarantees survival.

## Social hooks
- Shared panic: 30 players watching the same building fall.
- Random outcomes create stories ("the tsunami hit the tower I was on").
- No skill barrier lets friends of any age play together.

## Update cadence
Rare; updated 2026-09-17. The game survives on its format, not on content drops.

## Why it grew
- One of the earliest games on Roblox, built on the engine's physics strengths (destructible parts).
- Randomized map x disaster combinations give high replay value from small content.
- Very short rounds fit any session length.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Combine two random lists.** N maps x M disasters gives N*M rounds from small content; the same trick works for modifiers x arenas.
2. **Use the engine's strengths.** Roblox physics destruction is free spectacle.
3. **Cheap, fun, spawn-item passes.** 60-95 Robux helpers are impulse buys that never break the round.
4. **Announce the threat.** Telling players what is coming gives a few seconds of planning and makes deaths feel fair.
5. **A one-line pitch.** If the rule fits in one sentence, kids explain it to friends.
