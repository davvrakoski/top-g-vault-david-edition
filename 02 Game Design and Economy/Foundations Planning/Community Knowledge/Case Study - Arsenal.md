# Case Study - Arsenal

Arsenal (ROLVe) is an arcade shooter where every kill swaps your weapon for the next one in a rotation, and the first player through the full list wins (a Gun Game format). It matters because it was the first Roblox FPS to 1 billion visits and shows how a fair shooter monetizes almost only with characters, effects and bundles.

## Snapshot (Roblox page, seen 2026-10-25)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Visits | 6.32 billion |
| Playing | ~3.8k |
| Favorites | 11.5 million |
| Likes | ~89% of 5.8M votes |
| Server size | 16 |
| Created / updated | 2015-08-18 / 2026-10-22 |

First FPS on Roblox to reach 1 billion visits (June 2020); won Game of the Year, Best Sound Design and Best Game Trailer at the 7th Bloxys and Best Shooter Experience at the 2024 Innovation Awards (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. Spawn with weapon 1 in the rotation.
2. Each kill advances you to the next weapon (from bazookas to spell books, per the page).
3. Final weapon is a melee; land the kill to win the round.
4. Earn BattleBucks and XP; spend on cosmetics.
Six modes are listed by Wikipedia (Standard, Competitive, Randomizer, Gun Rotation, Railgun Royale, Concussion Mania).

## Session shape
Rounds of a few minutes, instant respawn, continuous matchmaking: ideal for short sessions.

## Progression and economy
- No power progression: everyone gets the same weapons in order.
- BattleBucks buy skins, characters, melees, kill effects and announcers.
- Joining the ROLVe group gives a chat tag and 25% more BattleBucks and XP (page).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Five passes, all cosmetic: Primus the Knight Bundle (245), Day of the Dead Bundle (395), VIP (395: two exclusive characters and a kill effect), Bugs & Beasts Bundle (495, six characters), Nexus Bundle (725). Bundles group characters and melees by theme. Partnerships (Prime Gaming skins, 2022) add free cosmetics.

## Social hooks
- Kill effects and characters are seen by every opponent, so cosmetics advertise themselves.
- Live events (it was nominated for Best Live Event at the 8th Bloxys) and Roblox event participation (Egg Hunt 2019).

## Update cadence
Seasonal themed updates and events; the page was updated three days before this snapshot. Live player count today is far below its peak era, which shows how hard it is to hold a shooter audience as newer ones arrive (compare Rivals).

## Why it grew
- Gun Game is easy to understand and every round shows players a variety of weapons.
- Strong trailer and sound (award wins), plus heavy YouTube presence in 2019-2020.
- Fair rules: no pay-to-win weapons in the rotation.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Use a rule that shows off content.** Weapon rotation makes every match a tour of the arsenal.
2. **Sell themed bundles.** Grouping characters plus melee by theme lifts average purchase size.
3. **Make cosmetics visible to victims.** Kill effects and finisher melees are seen by the people you beat.
4. **Group-join multiplier.** +25% currency for joining the group builds a notify list cheaply.
5. **Fresh rivals will come.** Plan new modes and seasons, or a newer shooter will take your audience.
