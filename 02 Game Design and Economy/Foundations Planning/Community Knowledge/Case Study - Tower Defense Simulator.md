# Case Study - Tower Defense Simulator

Tower Defense Simulator (Paradoxum Games) is a co-op tower defense game against zombie waves, with a level-based tower unlock ladder and many limited event towers. It matters because it shows the cleanest version of "unlock by level OR buy early" monetization and how numbered versions plus event chapters keep a 2019 game current.

## Snapshot (Roblox page, seen 2026-10-25)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| Playing | ~20k |
| Favorites | 3.5 million |

## Core loop
1. Queue with up to a few friends on a map and difficulty.
2. Place towers with match cash; upgrade them between waves.
3. Survive waves and bosses; earn coins and XP.
4. Level up to unlock new towers; buy towers with coins; equip a loadout.
5. Harder modes and events need better loadouts.

## Session shape
One match is a set of waves (length depends on mode; roughly tens of minutes, unverified). Lobby-to-match loop is quick, so "one more game" is easy.

## Progression and economy
- Account level gates towers (for example Crook Boss at level 30, Turret at 50, Mortar at 75, Mercenary Base at 150, per pass texts).
- Coins buy standard towers; event currency buys event towers and skins.
- Crates, a battle pass and skins form the cosmetic layer (page changelog).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
21 passes. The pattern is clear:
- **Skip the level gate**: Crook Boss (225, "achieve level 30 or get it early"), Turret (340), Mortar (300), Mercenary Base (1,350), Pursuit (1,500), Gatling Gun (1,949).
- **Premium towers**: Cowboy (340), Saboteur (449), Warden (450), Engineer (1,690), Hacker (2,249).
- **Limited returns**: Slasher (499), Harvester (799), Executioner (799).
- **Fun and QoL**: Meme Emotes (55), Resize (70), DJ music (85), VIP (375: map voting, auto wave skip), Sandbox Plus (7,999).
- Group join gives +$100 starting cash (page).

## Social hooks
- Co-op matches need team composition (farms, support, damage).
- VIP map voting gives buyers a visible, harmless perk.
- Codes for like milestones (2 million likes, free skin) reward the community for page actions.

## Update cadence
Numbered semantic versions on the page (2.14 -> 2.16) shipped as "Night I/II/III" chapters of one event, each with a crate or battle pass.

## Why it grew
- Tower defense is a proven, readable genre that plays well on mobile.
- Long unlock ladder and regular events kept a 2019 game in the top charts for years (stats above).
- Clear, honest pass text: every level-skip pass says which level unlocks it for free.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **"Earn it at level X or buy it now."** Selling early access to items that are free later is easy to justify and keeps free players hopeful.
2. **Split an event into chapters.** Night I/II/III releases turn one event into three returns.
3. **Bring limited items back.** Returning event towers sell to new players and keep old ones feeling special.
4. **Reward page actions with codes.** Like and favorite milestones become a community goal.
5. **Give VIP a social-but-fair perk.** Map voting matters to everyone and breaks nothing.
