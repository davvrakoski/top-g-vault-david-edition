# Case Study - Blade Ball

Blade Ball (published by the "Wiggity." group; Naavik names the two developers as Y1ddy and Chunchbunch) is a one-button PvP dodgeball: a homing ball targets a player, who must parry it at the right time to send it to someone else; it speeds up until someone misses. It matters because it shows a tiny, borrowed mechanic going viral through TikTok, then staying on top through weekly updates, abilities and cosmetic cases.

## Snapshot (Roblox page, seen 2026-10-25)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Visits | 6.51 billion |
| Playing | ~19.8k |
| Favorites | 13.1 million |
| Likes | ~93% of 9.1M votes |
| Server size | 16 |

Reached 1 billion visits in 62 days, beating Piggy's 83 (Pocket Tactics). Naavik (2023-10-24) reports a peak over 418k CCU (RoMonitor), a jump from ~15k to 100k+ CCU between 6 and 9 September 2023 driven by TikTok, and 850M views on #bladeball at the time.

## Core loop
1. Round starts; the ball picks a target.
2. Press block (F / tap) at the right moment to deflect.
3. Ball speeds up each hit; misses eliminate players.
4. Last player standing wins coins; buy or spin for abilities and cosmetics.

## Session shape
Very short duels (Naavik cites 15-second duels) and rounds of a couple of minutes; ~10-minute average sessions (Rollimons via Naavik). Mid-round joiners wait to play.

## Progression and economy
- Abilities (dash, shields and more) are bought with coins or won from a paid spin.
- Naavik notes slow early earning (7 coins per knockout vs ~300 for the first ability).
- Swords, explosions and finishers are the cosmetic chase; weekly limited items.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Four passes: Trading Sign (112), Instant Spin (120, skip the crate animation), VIP (240: finisher effect, custom slash color), Double Coins (240). The developer told Naavik ~90% of revenue came from Robux purchases and premium case rolls (paid cosmetic loot boxes).

## Social hooks
- Every round is a spectator event: everyone watches the final duel.
- Finisher effects show your cosmetic to the whole server at the best moment.
- The team paid cash rewards for the most-viewed videos (Naavik); the Discord passed 500k members.

## Update cadence
Weekly limited items and frequent modes (Naavik). Copycats appeared quickly (Soccer Ball, Anime Ball, a Fortnite version), and constant updates kept Blade Ball ahead.

## Why it grew
- Mechanic came from existing games (TF2/Overwatch-style dodgeball modes, Roblox's Deflect); Blade Ball polished it and hit the right moment.
- $1,500 of early ad funding from an investor (Naavik), then TikTok clips and the Roblox algorithm (retention, "Continue" sort).
- One button: anyone on any device can play in seconds.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Timing mechanics are perfect for clips.** A last-second parry is a 5-second video.
2. **Show cosmetics at the climax.** Finishers and slash effects sell because the whole server watches the last hit.
3. **Reward creators directly.** Paying for top videos is cheap user acquisition.
4. **Ship weekly to beat copycats.** A simple mechanic is easy to clone; update speed is the moat.
5. **Watch early grind.** A slow first ability unlock hurts; consider a faster first reward.
