# Case Study - Steal a Brainrot

Steal a Brainrot (SpyderSammy; owned by DoBig Studios) is an idle tycoon where you buy meme characters that generate money in your base, and other players can run in and steal them. It matters because it holds the all-time single-game concurrency record, and its core twist (your income can be stolen) shows how one social conflict rule can turn an idle game into a frantic one.

## Snapshot (Roblox page, seen 2026-10-25)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Visits | 74.2 billion |
| Playing | ~297k |
| Favorites | 29.6 million |
| Likes | ~85% of 18.2M votes |
| Server size | 8 |
| Created / updated | 2025-05-16 / 2026-10-25 |

Record: 25.4 million concurrent users reported 2025-10-07, first game past 25M (PocketGamer.biz); the only game past 25M and the second past 20M after Grow a Garden (Wikipedia list). PocketGamer.biz notes its average playtime was about one third of Grow a Garden's. Won Best Creative Direction at the 2025 Innovation Awards (press), which was controversial because of reported AI use in creative assets.

## Core loop
From the page's how-to:
1. Buy a Brainrot from a conveyor of characters passing by.
2. It sits in your base and generates money over time.
3. Steal Brainrots from other players' bases (carry them home before you are hit).
4. Defend your base (timed locks, per community guides).
5. Rebirth for multipliers; buy slaps and troll gear.

## Session shape
Short, intense bursts: steal, run, lock, collect. Small servers (8) mean everyone is your neighbor and your thief.

## Progression and economy
- Rarity ladder of characters (higher rarity = more income).
- Rebirths reset for multipliers.
- Stealing is a direct transfer between players, a sink for one player and a source for another.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Three passes: 2x Money (299), VIP (375), Admin Commands (7,999). The admin pass sells server-level trolling power (type ;cmds). Most revenue is reported to come from in-game items like slap tools and character purchases with Robux (Developer Products; unverified).

## Social hooks
- Theft creates instant rivalry and revenge loops.
- Viral clips of upset children losing their Brainrots spread on social media (Wikipedia list).
- Meme characters (Italian brainrot) were already trending, so the theme came with an audience.

## Update cadence
Frequent events (a "Yin Yang" event around the October 2025 peak is cited by trackers, unverified); updated on the day of this snapshot.

## Why it grew
- Riding a live meme trend with a game built in its image.
- Stealing is easy to understand and creates drama for video.
- Part of the 2025 surge: PocketGamer.biz notes platform CCU at a record 47.4M in August 2025 and DAU up 41% in Q2.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Let players threaten each other's progress.** A theft or raid rule turns an idle loop into active play; add defenses so it is fair.
2. **Use conveyor shops.** Items moving past on a belt create urgency and a shared moment.
3. **Theme on a live trend, fast.** Memes fade; ship in weeks, not months.
4. **Expect emotional backlash.** Stealing from kids makes viral clips and also complaints; design clear protection windows.
5. **Short sessions can still win.** Lower average playtime than Grow a Garden still set the CCU record.
