# Case Study - Dress to Impress

Dress to Impress (Dress To Impress Group, created by a teenage developer known as Gigi) is a round-based fashion contest: everyone gets a theme, has about five minutes to build an outfit, then walks a runway and is rated 1-5 stars by the other players. It matters because it turns a dress-up toy into a competitive party game with voting, and shows how a round structure and themes create endless short-video content.

Initially released November 2023, surged in mid-2024 (2.7B visits then); partnered with Charli XCX (Brat, August 2024) and Lady Gaga (August 2025, "Mayhem" theme); three Innovation Awards (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. A theme is revealed (random).
2. Dressing phase (~5 minutes): clothes, hair, makeup, accessories.
3. Runway: each player walks and poses.
4. Everyone rates 1-5 stars; top placements earn currency and leaderboard rank.
5. Spend money on new items; repeat.

## Session shape
Fixed-length rounds of a few minutes; easy to play two or ten in a row.

## Progression and economy
- Money from placements buys items; levels and leaderboards track success.
- Quests and collaborations add special items (a Flee the Facility crossover quest in January 2026, per Wikipedia; "NEW QUEST" in the current title).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
14 passes: Increased Item Limit (129), Run Faster (149), themed sets (French Luxury 279; Moongazer, Denim, Rich Girl 299; Sweet Berry 349; Moonlight Butterfly 379; Queen of Hearts 399; Haunting Beauty 449), Materials+ (299), Custom Makeup (349), x2 Money (399), VIP Pass (799, VIP room). Sets are the main product: curated outfits that help in themed rounds.

## Social hooks
- Voting by peers is the whole game: you dress for an audience.
- Themes generate content prompts: "dressing as X in DTI" videos spread on TikTok.
- Celebrity collabs (Charli XCX, Lady Gaga as a judge) bring outside audiences.

## Update cadence
Frequent item drops and quests; updated on the day of this snapshot.

## Why it grew
- A deep fashion game was missing on Roblox (the creator's reported reasoning; unverified interview).
- Short timed rounds plus peer voting create tension and social comparison.
- TikTok trends around themes and poses carried it in 2024.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Add a timer and a vote to a sandbox.** Turning free creation into a short contest gives it stakes.
2. **Random prompts are content generators.** Each theme is a new video idea for creators.
3. **Sell curated sets.** A themed bundle is easier to buy than 12 single items.
4. **Use the runway as the reveal.** A structured show moment makes cosmetics visible to everyone.
5. **Plan anti-troll voting.** Peer rating needs safeguards (outlier dampening, reporting) or it gets gamed.
