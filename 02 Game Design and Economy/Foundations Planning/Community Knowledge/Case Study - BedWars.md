# Case Study - BedWars

BedWars (Easy.gg) is the Roblox version of the Minecraft minigame: teams defend a bed on a floating island while gathering resources to buy gear, and you only respawn while your bed stands. It matters for its monetization model (dozens of character kits at one price, plus a seasonal battle pass) and for its fixed weekly update slot.

One of the most played Roblox games in 2022 (Wikipedia list). The same studio shut down Rift Royale in August 2022 after exploiters made it unmaintainable (Wikipedia list), a lesson in itself.

## Core loop
1. Spawn on your team island next to your bed.
2. Generators produce iron, then diamonds and emeralds on shared islands.
3. Buy blocks, weapons, armor and team upgrades.
4. Bridge to enemies, break their beds, eliminate them.
5. Use your kit's ability to play a style (miner, archer, builder, healer...).

## Session shape
Matches of several minutes; 10+ modes (Lucky Block, SkyWars, Infected and more, per the page) and a Creative mode with optional scripting.

## Progression and economy
- Match economy resets every game (fair start).
- Account progression: kits, levels, battle pass tiers, cosmetics.
- Clans (a Clan Pass enables creating one).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
50 passes. 46 are kits at a flat 399 Robux (Miner, Barbarian, Archer, Builder, Melody healer, Cyber drones, and many more), plus a Clan Pass (399). Then VIP Rank (400), Halloween 2026 Battle Pass (799, kit at tier 35 plus skins) and a two-kit bundle (799). Some kits are free through events (Eldric, Crypt via Halloween events, per pass text). Kits change playstyle, which some players see as pay-to-win; that may explain part of the lower like ratio (unverified).

## Social hooks
- Team play with roles (defender, rusher, resource runner).
- Clans and VIP prefixes.
- Creative mode lets players host their own games.

## Update cadence
A fixed weekly slot printed on the page (Friday afternoon PDT), plus seasonal battle passes and parts ("[PART 2]" in the title).

## Why it grew
- Brought a famous Minecraft mode to Roblox's younger, mobile-heavy audience.
- Kits add variety and identity to a familiar format.
- Weekly updates build a ritual.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Publish your update time.** A fixed weekly slot trains players and creators to show up.
2. **Flat-price character kits.** One price for all kits makes the store simple; new kits become new revenue each week.
3. **Give some kits free via events.** It softens pay-to-win perception and rewards active players.
4. **Reset the match economy.** In-match resources from zero keep each game fair even with kits.
5. **Plan anti-exploit before launch.** Easy.gg's own Rift Royale was shut down by exploiters.
