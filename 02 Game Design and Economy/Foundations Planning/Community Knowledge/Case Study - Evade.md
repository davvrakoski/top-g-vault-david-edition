# Case Study - Evade

Evade (Hexagon Development Community) is a movement survival game: run, slide and bunny-hop away from "Nextbots" (meme-themed chaser NPCs that get louder as they close in), revive downed teammates and survive the round. It matters because it took a Garry's Mod mode and made movement mastery the core skill, then built a strong cosmetic economy from community-made sets.

Created 2022-06-10 by the group owned by OsterDog; based on the Garry's Mod Nextbot chase mode. A Sketch video on 2022-08-19 passed 500k views in two weeks; by 2022-08-30 the game had 115M visits (Know Your Meme).

## Core loop
1. Round starts on a map with Nextbots.
2. Move: sprint, slide, jump chains; movement tech is the skill.
3. When a teammate is downed, risk a revive.
4. Survive the timer or be the last team standing; earn XP and currency.
5. Spend on emotes, carries, deployables and utility items.

## Session shape
Rounds of a few minutes; quick re-queue. The page warns of flashing lights and loud sounds.

## Progression and economy
- XP and tokens; deployable and utility upgrades (current page update).
- Weekly missions grant exotic items and currencies; a Voucher currency for quick revives (page).
- A battle pass and a revamped shop (page).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
26 passes, a broad cosmetic catalog: emote packs (350-500), music carry packs (350), +6 emote slots (300), a RELICS Boombox UGC tie-in (300) with paid "add-on" auras (100-1,200) that require it, and many "community-created" cosmetic sets (400-600, e.g. Classic Roblox, Crystalline, Military), plus a limited return Eclipse Set (500, off sale Oct 31). Community-made sets are both content and marketing.

## Social hooks
- Revives create team play and heroic saves.
- Meme Nextbots spread on TikTok (Know Your Meme cites a music trend).
- Carrying a teammate with a music carry is a social flex.

## Update cadence
Seasonal events (Halloween now, with a 1.5x tag in the title), weekly missions, and a codebase rewrite for stability noted on the page.

## Why it grew
- A ready-made format from another platform with a strong meme identity.
- Movement skill gives depth and creator showcase clips.
- Free to play with no power purchases.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Make movement the skill.** A great movement kit gives depth without balance-sensitive weapons.
2. **Revives create teamwork.** Downed-not-dead states invite risk and rescue.
3. **Sell community-made cosmetic sets.** Creators make content, you curate and sell, and they promote it.
4. **Use add-ons that need a base item.** A base pass plus optional upgrades (the boombox + auras model) builds a ladder.
5. **Warn about intensity.** Loud chase audio is the hook; flash and sound warnings protect players (and your ratings).
