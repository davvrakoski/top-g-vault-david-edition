# Case Study - Work at a Pizza Place

Work at a Pizza Place (Dued1) is a cooperative job game: players pick roles (cashier, cook, boxer, delivery, supplier, manager) and keep a pizza shop running, then spend paychecks on houses and furniture. It matters because it is one of the oldest still-popular games on Roblox and its creator credits its success to encouraging socializing; it is a model for "job roles that need each other".

First released 2007-11-03; first game on Roblox to reach 100 million plays; 5 billion plays by February 2025 (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. Choose a job station.
2. Do the station task (take an order, make the pizza, box it, deliver it, restock supplies).
3. Earn pay per task plus a paycheck; the manager role oversees and can reward workers (community wiki detail).
4. Spend Moneyz on your house, furniture and vehicles.

## Session shape
Flexible: work a few orders, then go home and decorate, or hang out. Each task takes seconds.

## Progression and economy
- Money comes from work; houses and furniture are the sink.
- No levels; the "progress" is your home.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
15 passes, a wide price ladder:
- Fun/tools: Hang Glider (50), Emoji Pack (55), Golden Pizza Slicer (60), Manager Teleporter (80), Radio (120).
- Ownership: Garage with a private car (149), Delivery Vehicle (200), Animal Morph (200), Victorian Bundle (300), Pontoon Boat (650), Autumn Bundle (699).
- Housing: Large House (730), VIP (800: 50% more pay, 2x daily checks), Mansion (1,700, 800 furniture pieces), Estate (3,200).
Housing tiers act as the premium ladder; the most expensive passes are homes, the game's main sink.

## Social hooks
- Roles depend on each other: a cook with no supplier stalls, so players talk.
- The manager role creates mild social hierarchy and roleplay.
- Visitable homes.

## Update cadence
Light, seasonal (an Autumn bundle is live); updated 2026-10-21.

## Why it grew
- Teamwork with clear roles was rare in 2007 and remains readable today.
- Low skill floor; jobs are learnable in seconds.
- Housing gives long-term goals to a short task loop.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Design interlocking jobs.** Each role should feed the next, so cooperation happens without voice chat.
2. **Make the home the sink.** Pay earned in a social job loop is spent on self-expression.
3. **Sell bigger homes as the top tier.** Large House -> Mansion -> Estate is a natural price ladder.
4. **Give one role soft authority.** A manager seat adds roleplay and a goal (the teleporter pass sells fast access to it).
5. **Keep tasks seconds long.** Short, repeatable actions let players chat while working.
