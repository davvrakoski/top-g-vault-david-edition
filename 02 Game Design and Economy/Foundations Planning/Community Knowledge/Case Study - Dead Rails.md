# Case Study - Dead Rails

Dead Rails (RCM Games) is a co-op run where a group drives a steam train across a zombie-filled 1899 desert, scavenging fuel and loot along the way. It matters for any survival or zombie project: it shows how one moving base plus a clear "reach the end" goal turns a familiar zombie shooter into a fresh, very streamable format.

## Core loop
1. Board the train with your group in a lobby; launch a run.
2. The train needs fuel (coal or burnable items) to keep moving.
3. Stop at towns and points of interest to loot guns, ammo, bandages and sellable valuables.
4. Fight zombies and night threats; defend the train.
5. Reach the end of the line (the cure in Mexico, per the page story) or die trying; earn the meta currency (Bonds, per guides) to unlock classes and items.

## Session shape
One run is a single, self-contained session (roughly the length of a long match; exact length unverified). Day/night pacing gives natural tension peaks.

## Progression and economy
- In-run economy: fuel and loot space are the limits; sell valuables to buy gear.
- Meta progression: Bonds unlock classes and starting items between runs (community guides).

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Four passes: +5 Storage (95), Mauser C96 (148), Sawed-Off Shotgun (148), Tommy Gun (175). Each starting weapon pass only gives a better start; the run can still fail. Guides describe paid items as convenience rather than unfair advantage (third-party, unverified).

## Social hooks
- Co-op roles happen naturally: driver/stoker, looter, gunner.
- The train is a shared base everyone depends on, so friends must talk.
- Funny failure moments (running out of coal at night) are perfect short clips.

## Update cadence
Event updates (the page title shows "[EVENT]" now); updated the day of this snapshot.

## Why it grew
GameAnalytics breaks it down with the *Hit Makers* idea of "familiar with a twist": Western + horror + engineering. Distribution then did the rest:
- Late February 2025: Roblox's own TikTok, Instagram and X accounts promoted it (TikTok clip 499k+ likes).
- It reached the front page, Top Trending and Up-and-Coming lists.
- Large creators (Flamingo, KreekCraft, Thinknoodles, Sketch) covered it.
- Similar formula games: Build A Boat For Treasure, a dusty trip (moving base + hazards).

## Lessons you can copy
1. **A moving base is a co-op engine.** One shared vehicle that needs fuel gives every player a job and a reason to cooperate.
2. **Twist a familiar genre with a setting.** Zombies are common; zombies in the 1899 Wild West on rails are a headline.
3. **Starter-kit passes keep runs fair.** Selling a better first gun shortens the weak opening without guaranteeing a win.
4. **Design for the clip.** Day/night swings and resource panic create the moments short videos need.
5. **Pitch to the platform.** Official Roblox social posts and the trending sorts were the inflection point; make a game that is easy to show in 15 seconds.
