# Case Study - Bee Swarm Simulator

Bee Swarm Simulator (Onett, solo developer) is a collection simulator: your bees follow you, gather pollen from fields, you convert it to honey at your hive, and you spend honey on more bees and gear. It matters because it has the best like ratio in this set (~96%) and shows that a simulator can earn deep loyalty through quests, hand-made characters and slow, careful updates instead of fast content churn.

Released to the public 2018-03-23 (community wiki). Took part in the 2020 Egg Hunt and several 2024 Roblox events (Wikipedia list).

## Core loop
1. Stand in a flower field; bees collect pollen into your backpack.
2. Return to the hive and convert pollen to honey.
3. Spend honey on eggs (new bees), tools, backpacks and hive slots.
4. Do quests from bear NPCs for big rewards.
5. Unlock higher fields further up the mountain (zone gates by bee count, per the page: "as your hive grows, you can explore further").

## Session shape
Long, relaxed farming sessions; quests break them into goals. Small servers (6) mean fields are rarely crowded.

## Progression and economy
- Bee types have traits and personalities (page); rarity tiers and gifted variants (community wiki).
- Quest chains from many NPC bears are the main progression guide; Wikipedia's entry calls out quests and events as the hook.
- Mobs and bosses guard rewards; dispensers and timed items create daily routines.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
No passes currently for sale. Monetization is mainly through the in-game premium currency (tickets, per community wiki; unverified for the current version) sold as Developer Products. Notably restrained compared with other simulators in this set.

## Social hooks
- The "Bee Swarm Simulator Club" group linked on the page.
- Event seasons (Beesmas is the famous winter event, per community wiki) create shared anticipation.
- Bear characters with distinct personalities give the community lore to talk about.

## Update cadence
Infrequent but large: the page was last updated 2026-08-22, two months before this snapshot. Long gaps did not destroy the audience because players have long quest chains to finish.

## Why it grew
- Bees following you around is a charming, readable fantasy.
- Quest-driven structure gives direction that most 2018 simulators lacked.
- Solo dev with consistent quality; the game feels hand-crafted.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Put progression in quests from characters.** NPC quest chains teach, pace and add personality in one system.
2. **Make your helpers visible.** Followers (bees, pets) that act in the world beat numbers in a menu.
3. **Gate zones by collection size.** "Have N bees to enter" ties zone access to the core collection.
4. **Restraint can be a brand.** A light store and high quality earn a 96% like ratio.
5. **One signature event.** A recurring yearly event becomes a community holiday.
