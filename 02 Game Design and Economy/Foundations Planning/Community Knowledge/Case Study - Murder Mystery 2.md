# Case Study - Murder Mystery 2

Murder Mystery 2 (Nikilis) is a round-based social deduction game: one Murderer, one Sheriff, everyone else Innocent. It matters because a 2014 game with simple rounds still sits near the top of the platform, carried by a knife and gun collection economy that is traded like currency.

## Core loop
1. Join a round; roles are assigned at random.
2. Murderer must eliminate everyone with a knife (melee or thrown); Sheriff has the only gun.
3. If the Sheriff dies, the gun drops and an Innocent can take it (hero moment).
4. Innocents collect coins on the map during the round.
5. Coins buy mystery boxes that drop knife and gun skins; collect and trade.

## Session shape
Rounds of a few minutes with a short lobby gap; perfect for 10-30 minute sessions. Roles change every round, so each round feels different.

## Progression and economy
- Levels and XP; prestige (community wiki detail, unverified).
- Knife and gun skins by rarity (the page says "hundreds of knives"); Godly and Ancient tiers are the chase items (community wiki).
- Trading is huge: players treat rare knives as a store of value; fan value lists exist outside the game.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
Three passes: Radio (475), Elite (499: 1.5x XP, tag, exclusive knife, coin bag limit raised to 50), and a limited Mortal bundle (3,399: exclusive Godly knife and effect). Mystery boxes and Robux-only weapon sets are sold in-game (Developer Products). The Elite pass raising the coin bag cap is a clean "earn more per round" perk.

## Social hooks
- Role reveal moments and accusations create chat drama.
- Codes posted on the creator's socials ("free knife codes" on the page) drive follows.
- Showing a rare knife as the Murderer is the flex.

## Update cadence
Seasonal events (Halloween and Christmas in particular, community wiki) with limited-time weapons; updated 2026-10-21.

## Why it grew
- Simple roles anyone understands; works on every device.
- Random roles give everyone a chance to be the main character.
- The weapon collection made it a trading game layered on a party game, which keeps players for years.

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Rotate roles every round.** Random asymmetric roles make one map replayable for a decade.
2. **Give the losing side a comeback.** The dropped gun turns any Innocent into a hero.
3. **Cap per-round earnings, sell the cap.** A coin bag limit stops AFK farming and gives a fair pass to sell.
4. **Let cosmetics show in the key moment.** The Murderer's knife is seen at the kill.
5. **Seasonal limited items build long-term trade value.** Old event items become prized.
