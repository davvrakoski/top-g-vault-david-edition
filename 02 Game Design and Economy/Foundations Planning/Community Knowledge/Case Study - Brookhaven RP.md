# Case Study - Brookhaven RP

Brookhaven RP is the most visited game in this set: a free-form town where players make their own stories with houses, cars and props. It matters because it proves a game with almost no goals can top the platform if it is a great stage for player-made fun, and it shows how to sell "more stage" instead of power.

Created by Wolfpaq; acquired by Voldex on 2025-02-05 with funding from Raine Partners and Shamrock Capital (Wikipedia list). Once peaked near 800k concurrent players (Wikipedia list). Won Best Roleplay & Avatar Sim at the 2025 Roblox Innovation Awards (press reports).

## Core loop
There is no fixed loop. Players: pick a house, change avatar/role, grab a vehicle or tool, then act out scenes (family, police, school, horror) with friends or strangers. The "loop" is social improvisation.

## Session shape
Drop-in, no tutorial wall, no fail state. Sessions follow the group: join a friend, play out a scene, leave. Low commitment is the feature.

## Progression and economy
Almost none by design. Seasonal events add collectibles (the Halloween event: collect Candy Corn to unlock tools, props, vehicles and a house, per the page). Everything else is unlocked by passes.

## Monetization (store page, 2026-10-25)
18 passes, 35 to 749 Robux, all expanding the stage:
- Cheap impulse: Vehicle Upgrade (35), On Demand Fire (40), Horse Upgrade (85).
- Mid: Penthouse (180), Vehicle Speed/Boost/Music (199 each), Customization, Boat Pack, Theme Pack (299 each), Premium (320), Land Unlocked and Disaster Pass (375).
- Big: Estates, Vehicle Pack, House Pets (599 each), Prison Landmark (699), VIP (749, "100+ exclusive items").
Private servers are sold with a long admin feature list: prop saving, server-wide music, kick/ban, weather, time, themes.

## Social hooks
- Every pass makes the buyer's scene better for everyone watching (a disaster, a theme, a mansion), so purchases are visible and shared.
- Private servers turn the game into a toolkit for friend groups and content creators.

## Update cadence
Seasonal events and new lots/vehicles; the page was updated two days before this snapshot.

## Why it grew
- Released in April 2020, during lockdown, as a cleaner, simpler take on town roleplay.
- Zero skill floor and full mobile support suit the youngest players.
- Endless short-video and YouTube "story" content is made inside it, which acts as free marketing (unverified as a measured cause).

## Lessons you can copy
1. **Sell stage, not power.** Themes, disasters, lots and vehicles change the scene for the whole server and never break fairness.
2. **Make private servers a product.** Give VIP server owners real admin tools; it converts groups and creators.
3. **Keep a price ladder.** Brookhaven has entries at 35-85, 180-375 and 599-749 Robux, so every budget has an option.
4. **Remove fail states for social games.** No timer, no loss means players stay to talk and play pretend.
5. **Seasonal collectibles for a goal-less game.** A light "collect X to unlock" event gives regulars a reason to return.
