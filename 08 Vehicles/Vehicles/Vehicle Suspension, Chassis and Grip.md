# Vehicle Suspension, Chassis & Grip

A good Roblox vehicle is a controlled physics system, not just four spinning wheels.

## Tune in order
1. mass distribution and center of mass,
2. suspension travel and spring strength,
3. damping,
4. tire/grip behavior,
5. steering response,
6. acceleration/braking,
7. camera and audio.

Changing everything at once makes tuning impossible. Create a repeatable test track with flat road, bumps, curb, hill, slalom and emergency braking.

## Stability
Keep the center of mass believable but low enough for the intended handling style. Prevent unrealistic angular impulses from tiny debris. Test at low and high frame/network conditions.

## Lab
Record 0–top-speed time, braking distance, slalom time and rollover behavior before and after each tuning pass.
