## Checked and left out

Source | Why
---|---
Quaternius | its license bans reselling or redistributing the assets themselves, even inside a bundle
Mixamo | Adobe terms forbid redistributing raw animation files
CMU motion capture | free to use, but resale of the data is not allowed
Small GitHub repos marked CC0 | provenance not verifiable; linked from notes, not bundled

## Honest selling checklist
- Say clearly in the listing that the asset library is CC0 / free-to-use content, curated and organised.
- Keep each `License.txt` file in its folder.
- Never add assets whose license you have not read.
