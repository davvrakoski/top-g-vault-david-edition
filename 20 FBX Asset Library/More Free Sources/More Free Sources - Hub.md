## How to use these files in Roblox
- Every source note has a preview table (triangles, Roblox ≤20k check, author, license) and Roblox import tips.
- Over-limit scans come with a ready `*_Roblox20k.fbx` (auto-decimated) next to the full-detail FBX.
- Keep `textures/` next to the FBX when importing; add a SurfaceAppearance if a map does not come in.

## Selling this bundle: what to keep in mind
- **CC BY 4.0 items (Khronos only)**: buyers must credit the authors. Keep each `LICENSE.txt` in the bundle and list the credit lines on the store page.
- **NASA items**: credit NASA; never use the NASA insignia/logotype as branding or imply NASA endorses the product. The same applies to markings on the Smithsonian aerospace scans (flagged in their LICENSE.txt).
- CC0 = no attribution needed, but keep the LICENSE.txt files: they are the provenance record if a store or platform asks.
