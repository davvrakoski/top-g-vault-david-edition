# MONETIZATION

=================================================

GOOD:
- cosmetics
- convenience
- meaningful passes
- optional progression accelerators that do not destroy fairness
- subscriptions if real recurring value exists
- clear prices
- honest descriptions

BAD:
- manipulative popups every minute
- fake urgency
- paywalls before player understands game
- misleading purchase prompts
- predatory design

TRACK:
conversion rate
paying users
ARPDAU
ARPPU
revenue by product
retention of purchasers vs non-purchasers
