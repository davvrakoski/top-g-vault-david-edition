# Developer Products, Passes & Subscriptions

Choose the monetization primitive based on what the player is buying: permanent entitlement, repeatable purchase or recurring value.

## Design rule
The purchase should have a clear description and reliable server-side fulfillment. Never grant permanent value only because the client says a prompt succeeded.

## Lab
Document each product with ownership model, fulfillment path, failure/retry behavior and analytics event.
