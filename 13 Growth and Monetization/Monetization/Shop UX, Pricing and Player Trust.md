# Shop UX, Pricing & Player Trust

A high-converting shop does not need to be manipulative.

Make prices, contents and permanence obvious. Avoid fake countdowns, misleading buttons and purchase prompts before the player understands the game.

Use analytics to understand conversion, but protect long-term trust and retention.

## Lab
Ask a tester to explain exactly what each offer gives before purchasing. If they cannot, rewrite the offer.
