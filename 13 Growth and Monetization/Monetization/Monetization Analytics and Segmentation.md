# Monetization Analytics & Segmentation

Track conversion, revenue by product, payer retention, ARPDAU/ARPPU where useful and the relationship between purchases and progression.

Segment by new/returning player and gameplay stage rather than only looking at one global number. A product that converts well but increases churn may be a bad product.

## Lab
Create a dashboard spec with one metric for acquisition, engagement, retention, conversion and economy health.
