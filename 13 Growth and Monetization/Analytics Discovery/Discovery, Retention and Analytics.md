# DISCOVERY, RETENTION & ANALYTICS

=================================================

Roblox Discovery currently emphasizes signals such as:
- play-through rate
- first-play bounce
- play days per user
- playtime
- qualified sessions
- social co-play
- monetization-related value

DO NOT TRY TO "GAME THE ALGORITHM."
Make the player have a good first session.

FIRST 60 SECONDS:
- fast load
- clear goal
- movement works
- no giant tutorial wall
- first reward quickly
- show what makes game unique

D1 RETENTION:
Why come back tomorrow?

D7:
What progression/content keeps mattering?

D30:
What long-term identity/social/mastery exists?

ANALYTICS EVENTS:
TutorialStarted
TutorialCompleted
FirstKill
FirstDeath
FirstReward
FirstPurchase
AreaUnlocked
SessionMilestone
BossAttempt
BossWin
QuitPoint

Use funnels to find where players leave.
