# Tutorial, Onboarding & FTUE

The first session should teach by doing.

## First-minute goals
Load quickly, establish movement, show the immediate goal, let the player succeed at something and reveal the game's unique hook.

## Progressive disclosure
Do not show a wall of every system. Teach controls when they become relevant. Let advanced menus stay quiet until players need them.

## Instrumentation
Measure tutorial start/completion, each step, first failure, first reward and quit point. Segment by device where possible.

## Recovery
Players who skip or forget need a help/settings route to relearn controls.

## Lab
Record five first-time players without coaching them. Every question they ask is evidence that something was not communicated clearly.
