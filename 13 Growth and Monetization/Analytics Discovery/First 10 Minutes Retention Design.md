# First 10 Minutes Retention Design

## First 10 seconds
- world visible quickly
- controls responsive
- clear immediate point of interest

## First minute
- player successfully does the core action
- receives feedback/reward
- sees a reason to continue

## First five minutes
- first meaningful choice
- progression becomes visible
- one memorable moment

## First ten minutes
- player understands loop
- has a next goal
- has seen something worth returning for

Avoid:
- giant text wall tutorial
- forced cutscene before player knows why they care
- opening shop popup before gameplay
- unexplained currencies everywhere
