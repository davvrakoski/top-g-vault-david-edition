# Thumbnail and Icon A/B Test Playbook

A thumbnail's job is not "be pretty." It must attract the right player **without lying about the game**.

## Three creative hypotheses
A. Character/emotion close-up.
B. Core gameplay action.
C. World/mystery/goal.

Keep branding consistent while changing one major idea at a time.

## Evaluate
- play-through rate from recommendations
- first-play bounce
- downstream session quality

A clickbait thumbnail that increases clicks but attracts the wrong audience can hurt because Roblox's discovery system also values whether users actually stay and return.

Current discovery documentation lists important signals including play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days per user, and playtime, with additional signals such as co-play, qualified sessions, and monetization-related value.

Sources:
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq
