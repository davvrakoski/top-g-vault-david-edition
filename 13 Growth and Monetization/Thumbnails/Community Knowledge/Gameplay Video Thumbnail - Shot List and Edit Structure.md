# Gameplay Video Thumbnail - Shot List and Edit Structure

Roblox lets you upload an authentic gameplay video that shows **first** on your game's detail page and can also appear on Home (not on Xbox, PlayStation or VR). You get **3 uploads per month** and rejected videos still use quota, so a sloppy first attempt costs a month's chances. The policy limits are covered in the thumbnail personalization deep dive; this note is the **production** side: what to shoot and how to cut it, using the advice of trailer editor Derek Lieu.

## The rule that matters most: gameplay in the first seconds

Lieu's best-known advice (as summarized by GameDiscoverCo and in a PC Gamer interview): start with a few seconds of real gameplay, because viewers skip logos and slow establishing shots. He builds trailers like a tutorial: show the main unique mechanic first, then expand on it. On Roblox this fits the policy perfectly, because logos-first intros, narration and claims are exactly what the rules restrict anyway.

## Shot list (plan before recording)

List your game's **verbs** (Lieu's method): run, jump, swing, hatch, build, escape. For each, plan one clean 2-4 s clip:

1. **Hook (0-3 s):** the most exciting core action, mid-action (boss swing, giant pet hatch, chase).
2. **Core loop (3-12 s):** 3-4 clips showing the loop in order (collect -> upgrade -> unlock).
3. **Progression payoff (12-20 s):** before/after of a base, a rare item, a new zone reveal.
4. **Social (20-25 s):** several players together, co-op moment, trading, a party.
5. **End (25-30 s):** wide shot of the world, logo overlay allowed (logo/branding overlay is explicitly OK).

Keep it short; a tight 20-30 s cut is easier to keep authentic and loops well on Home (target length is our suggestion, not a Roblox rule).

## Capturing in Studio

- Use a **capture copy** of the place with real systems on, debug UI off.
- **Freecam:** `Left Shift + P` in a solo playtest toggles free camera mode for cinematic angles (Roblox docs). Camera work is allowed if it shows real gameplay.
- Record at 1920x1080, 60 fps, with graphics at max; use friends or test accounts for multiplayer shots.
- Do every action for real. Curated highlight sequences are allowed; faked mechanics are not.

## Edit rules that keep you inside the policy

- Allowed: varied camera angles, cuts between real clips, minor brightness/contrast tweaks, in-game UI text, music from Roblox's audio catalog, logo overlay.
- Not allowed: voice-over or narration, voice chat, music with lyrics, real-world footage, enhanced graphics beyond what players see, ad claims ("50% off", "Free UGC!"). Overlay text only to describe gameplay ("Collect coins to boost jumps").
- Cut on action (a hit, a jump landing) and match the music beats.

## Before uploading

- [ ] First frame is gameplay, not a title.
- [ ] Every mechanic shown exists in the current build.
- [ ] No spoken audio, no lyrics, no claims.
- [ ] Watched muted on a phone: still understandable.
- [ ] Theme matches the game's genre and thumbnails (a horror game shows horror).
