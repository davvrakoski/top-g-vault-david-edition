# Advanced Thumbnail Production Pipeline

A thumbnail is a promise about the game.

## Pipeline
1. Define one player fantasy.
2. Capture or stage a scene that genuinely represents gameplay.
3. Select one dominant subject.
4. Control value/contrast before adding effects.
5. Use effects only to clarify action.
6. Check at tiny size.
7. Compare variants with analytics rather than opinions alone.

## Avoid AI-looking results
Over-smoothed characters, impossible lighting, excessive rim glow, hyper-detailed backgrounds, fake UI, inconsistent shadows and features not present in the game make a thumbnail feel deceptive.

## Variant strategy
Change one major hypothesis at a time: character emotion, camera distance, action, environment or title treatment. If every element changes, you learn nothing from the comparison.

## Lab
Make three truthful variants and write the hypothesis each tests before publishing any of them.
