# Discord Community Operations

A large Discord is part community, part support desk, part moderation system and part research channel.

## Structure
Keep announcements separated from general chat. Give bug reports and suggestions templates. Use role permissions so staff actions are auditable and dangerous permissions are rare.

## Feedback quality
Ask for device, reproduction steps, clip/screenshot, expected result and actual result. Upvotes alone do not prove a suggestion is good; combine frequency, severity and game vision.

## Release communication
For every update provide what changed, known issues and where to report problems. For incidents, communicate verified facts and next actions without guessing.

## Knowledge sharing
Turn repeated answers into FAQ/vault notes. The community becomes more useful when discoveries are documented instead of disappearing in chat history.

## Lab
Audit the top 20 repeated questions from the last month and convert them into searchable documentation.
