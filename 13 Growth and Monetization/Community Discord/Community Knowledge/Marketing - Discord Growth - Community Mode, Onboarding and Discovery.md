# Marketing - Discord Growth - Community Mode, Onboarding and Discovery

A Discord server grows from the outside in. People arrive from your videos, the game page's Social Links (16+ verified viewers only) and creator shout-outs. Whether they **stay** depends on the first 60 seconds: do they find the right channel, a reason to talk, and something happening this week? This note covers the growth side. Moderation and raid defense have their own notes, linked below.

## 1. Turn on Community mode first
Server Settings > **Enable Community**. It unlocks **Onboarding**, Server Insights, announcement channels (others can follow them into their servers), welcome screens, and eligibility for **Server Discovery**. Community mode requires some safety settings (verification level, explicit media filter, rules and updates channels).

## 2. Onboarding: route members on day 1 (Discord support article)
Server Settings > **Onboarding**:
1. **Default channels**, which everyone sees: #announcements, #general, #updates, #clips, #help. Discord's article suggests popular, welcoming channels.
2. **Customization questions**: each answer grants **channels and/or roles**. Example questions:
   - "What brings you here?" (Play the game / Watch dev progress / Make content / Report bugs)
   - "Which platform do you play on?" (PC / mobile / console)
   - "Ping me for..." (updates / playtests / code drops / events)
3. Members can change answers later under **Channels & Roles**. Use **Preview** before publishing.
4. Third-party guides say regular mode needs **≥ 7 default channels with ≥ 5 that @everyone can view and post in** **(verify in your settings screen)**.

Ping roles from question 3 let you announce without @everyone. Opt-in pings keep people from muting the server.

## 3. Weekly reasons to come back
| Ritual | Effort | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Clip of the week** (members post, you pick one) | low | user content plus recognition |
| **Dev sneak peek** every Friday | low | exclusive, and only here |
| **Playtest night** with devs in-game | medium | the strongest bond; feeds feedback |
| **Code drop** for members (also posted on-platform) | low | small reward, no follow-gating |
| **Poll** on the next feature | low | ownership |

Schedule them with Discord **Events** so they show at the top of the server.

## 4. Getting found: Server Discovery
Discord's article lists 6 requirements for Discovery, including at least **1,000 members**, a server **at least 8 weeks old**, **2FA required for moderators**, safety settings, a clean name and description, and meeting unstated **activity** thresholds. Until you reach that, growth comes from your content.

## 5. Funnel links correctly
- In-game: never paste the invite. Use "Join our community: links on the game page" (Roblox rule).
- Game page: **Social Links > Discord**, visible to age-verified 16+ users.
- Videos and bios: a **permanent vanity or never-expiring invite**. Create a separate invite per channel (TikTok, YouTube, creator X) and check **invite usage counts** in Server Settings > Invites. It's the Discord version of Share Link tracking.
- Verify members to Roblox accounts for roles (Bloxlink). See the linked note.

## 6. Signals to watch (Server Insights)
- New members who **talk in their first day**: if that's low, simplify onboarding.
- Join sources: which invite is working.
- Leaves after an announcement: you pinged too much.

## Pitfalls
- 40 empty channels on day one. Start with about 8 and add channels when one gets crowded.
- @everyone pings for minor news.
- Making Discord the **only** place for codes or updates. Under-16 players can't see the link on Roblox, so mirror key news on-platform.
