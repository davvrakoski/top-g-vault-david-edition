# Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform

Launch day is when every channel you've built fires at once. The usual failures are boring ones: links that point to the wrong place, servers full of players with nothing announced, a trailer with a lyric track rejected from the game page, or the dev busy firefighting while Discord has no information. Use this checklist **alongside** the technical release checklist. It covers marketing, comms and measurement only.

## T-7 days
- [ ] **Share Links** created for every channel (`tt_bio`, `yt_trailer`, `dc_announce`, `cr_<creator>`, `press_kit`) and logged in the sheet
- [ ] **Launch event** public, unique thumbnail, correct place; submitted for **Off-Platform Featuring** (needs ≥ 7 days' notice); RSVP push running
- [ ] **Today's Picks** nomination sent
- [ ] Game page ready: title, description (top 2 lines say what you do), icon, **thumbnails 1920×1080**, a gameplay **video** approved (no VO or lyrics; 3 uploads/month), **Social Links** set (owner verified 16+)
- [ ] Trailer in 3 cuts (16:9, 9:16, Roblox page); press kit live
- [ ] Creators briefed with disclosure rules; early-access server booked
- [ ] Codes created and **tested** in a live server; abuse limits on

## T-1 day
- [ ] All posts drafted and scheduled; captions proofread; links clicked from a **logged-out phone**
- [ ] Discord: #launch-faq pinned (how to join, known issues, how to report bugs); roles ready for pings
- [ ] Mod rota for the first 48 h, covering your players' time zones
- [ ] Crisis templates ready (servers full, outage, exploit). See the community playbook.
- [ ] Notifications off on your PC; OBS Replay Buffer on for clips

## Launch hour
- [ ] Game public / event live (check from an alt account)
- [ ] Post order: Discord announcement, then TikTok/Shorts launch clip, then YouTube trailer premiere or live stream, then X/Bluesky announcement (link in reply), then the Roblox community announcement
- [ ] Creators go live within the agreed window
- [ ] Pin the best launch post on every profile

## First 6 hours
- [ ] Watch **live servers and error reports** before reading comments: technical problems first
- [ ] Post a status update every 2-3 h on Discord, even "all good, fixing X"
- [ ] Clip funny or epic launch moments with the Replay Buffer and post 1-2 same-day
- [ ] Reply to early comments (first-hour engagement helps short-video reach)

## Day 2-7
- [ ] Acquisition > **Share Links** view: plays, conversion, then D7 retention as it matures
- [ ] Update announcement only if something real changed (60 chars, once per 3 days)
- [ ] "Thank you + what's next" post with honest numbers or milestones
- [ ] Hot-fix notes in player language
- [ ] Debrief on day 7: which channel brought players who **stayed**; plan the next 4 weeks

## Don'ts
- Don't paste Discord or social links inside the game. Point to "links on the game page".
- Don't promise a fix time you can't hit.
- Don't run Robux giveaways or follow-to-unlock rewards (Community Standards).
- Don't post launch numbers you can't back up.
