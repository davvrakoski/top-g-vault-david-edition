# Marketing - YouTube Shorts for a Roblox Game - Specs, Related Video Links and Repurposing

YouTube is the only big short-video platform where a Short can send viewers straight to a **long-form video on your own channel**, and Roblox players already search YouTube for game guides. For a creator-dev that makes Shorts a funnel: Short, then devlog or guide, then subscriber, then player. This note covers what makes an upload count as a Short, how to link Shorts to long-form, and how to reuse TikTok edits without losing reach.

## 1. What YouTube counts as a Short (official)
- **Square or vertical** aspect ratio, **up to 3 minutes**, uploaded on or after **15 Oct 2024**. Older uploads keep their original classification.
- A 16:9 video is long-form at any length.
- Use 1080×1920 (9:16) for full-screen display. Square works but wastes screen space.
- 3-minute Shorts upload via the YouTube app and YouTube Studio.

Practical length: most game Shorts work best at **15-40 s** with a loop. Use the long cap for "tutorial in a Short" or a full challenge run.

## 2. Related video: the funnel feature
YouTube Studio lets you attach a **Related video** to a Short: Content, then the Short, then **Related video**, then pick a long-form video, livestream or another Short from your channel. It shows as a tappable link near the bottom of the Short.
- Point every devlog-bite Short at the **full devlog episode**.
- Point gameplay Shorts at a **"how to play / beginner guide"** video whose description has the game's Share Link (`yt_guide_*`).
- Update the link when a newer episode comes out.

## 3. Repurposing TikTok edits
1. Export a **clean master** without TikTok watermarks, captions burned in by TikTok, or TikTok-only stickers. Keep your own text overlays in the project file.
2. Re-time the first 2 seconds if needed. A YouTube Shorts viewer swipes just as fast.
3. Re-check music: a sound that's fine inside TikTok's library isn't licensed on YouTube. Use your game soundtrack or YouTube Audio Library tracks.
4. Title: under ~60 characters, keyword first ("Roblox zombie mall survival - boss fight").
5. Description: one line plus the **Share Link** (Shorts descriptions are rarely opened, so the Related video link does more).

## 4. Reading Shorts results
In YouTube Analytics, open **"How viewers find your Shorts"** (Shorts feed, search, channel pages, external). Watch:
- **Viewed vs swiped away**: hook quality. Under half viewed means re-cut the opening.
- **Average % viewed above 100%** means people rewatched, so the loop worked.
- **Subscribers gained per Short**: which formats build the channel.
- Then go to Roblox Acquisition and check the Share Link plays for `yt_*` links. Views don't count as players.

## 5. Made for kids and paid promotion
- Set the audience honestly. Most Roblox dev content is general audience, but content clearly aimed at young children is "made for kids" under YouTube's COPPA rules, and that limits features. See [[Marketing - Marketing to Kids and Teens - CARU, Made for Kids and Age Rules]].
- Paid collabs: tick **"Yes, my video includes branded content"** (YouTube's current wording) **and** disclose on screen.

## Checklist
- [ ] 9:16 1080×1920, ≤ 3 min, loop-friendly ending
- [ ] Related video set on every Short
- [ ] Clean master re-exported with YouTube-safe audio
- [ ] Share Link in the long-form description the Short points to
- [ ] Weekly check: viewed vs swiped, subs per Short, `yt_*` share-link plays
