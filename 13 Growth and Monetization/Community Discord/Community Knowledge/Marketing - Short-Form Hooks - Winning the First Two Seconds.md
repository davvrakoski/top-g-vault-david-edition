# Marketing - Short-Form Hooks - Winning the First Two Seconds

On TikTok and YouTube Shorts the viewer's thumb is already moving. If the first frame doesn't give them a reason to stay, the rest of the edit doesn't matter. TikTok's own performance guidance asks for the **value proposition within the first 3 seconds** and the **hook within 6**. In practice the first **1-2 seconds** decide whether people swipe. This note is a hook toolkit for Roblox game clips. It applies to organic posts and paid ads alike.

## What TikTok's creative guide actually says (June 2025)
- Vertical **9:16**, at least **720p**, **sound on** (music or voice), key content inside the **UI safe zone**.
- Content proposition in the first **3 s**; hook in the first **6 s** using suspense, surprise or emotion.
- On-screen text at about **5-10 words per second**, at most.
- A native, lo-fi, "made by a person" look beats polished ad style. Use trends where they fit.
- End with a clear call to action.
- For ads, test **3-5 clearly different creatives** at once.

## Seven hook patterns that fit Roblox games

| Hook | First frame | Example caption |
|---|---|---|
| **Payoff first** | the explosion, boss kill or rare drop, *then* rewind | "this took me 3 hours to build" |
| **Impossible stat** | a big number on screen | "1 player vs 100 zombies" |
| **Question** | a player at a decision point | "would you open the red door?" |
| **Before/after** | ugly greybox, then hard cut to final | "my game day 1 vs day 90" |
| **Fail / chaos** | physics going wrong | "I did NOT code this" |
| **Challenge the viewer** | a hard obstacle | "99% of players fail this jump" (only if roughly true) |
| **Creator POV** | your face or hands with Studio on screen | "I'm making a Roblox game where..." |

Rules that make any hook stronger:
1. **Motion in frame 1.** No logo or title card, and no fade from black. GameDiscoverCo's trailer analysis makes the same point: viewers skip setup to reach gameplay.
2. **Text overlay of 3-7 words** that states the stakes, placed in the upper-middle third so it clears the platform UI.
3. **Sound starts at frame 1**: a punchy SFX, a voice line or the beat drop.
4. **One idea per clip.** If you need two ideas, make two videos.
5. **Loop it.** Cut the last frame back toward the first so a rewatch feels seamless. Replays help watch time.

## Write five hooks per clip
For every piece of footage, write five opening lines and five first frames, then pick the strongest pair. Post variants on different days (or as ad creatives) and keep what holds viewers. Look at the **retention graph**: a cliff in the first 2 seconds means the hook failed, while a slow decline means the middle dragged.

## Pitfalls
- Misleading hooks ("free admin!", fake features). They get clicks, the players who arrive feel cheated and leave, and Roblox video thumbnails forbid the same kind of claims.
- A dev-heavy hook ("I optimized my raycasts") draws other developers, not players. DevForum posters report the same. Save it for devlog channels.
- Starting with "Hey guys, today...". Cut it.
