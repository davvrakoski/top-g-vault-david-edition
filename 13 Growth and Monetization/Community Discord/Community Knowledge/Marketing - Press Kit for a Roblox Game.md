# Marketing - Press Kit for a Roblox Game

Journalists, YouTubers, brand partners and Roblox's own editorial teams all need the same things: what the game is, good images, the logo, a trailer and a contact. If they have to ask, many won't bother. A press kit is one public page with all of it. Rami Ismail, who created the widely used **presskit()** template, has said a press kit should be an easy-to-use resource rather than a pretty page. For a Roblox game it doubles as your **creator kit**.

## 1. Tools
- **presskit()** (dopresskit.com): Rami Ismail's free, open-source template, a de-facto indie standard. You edit XML files, and it needs PHP hosting.
- **presskit.html** (Pixelnest): an **MIT-licensed** re-implementation that builds **static HTML** with no PHP, so it can be hosted on GitHub Pages or similar.
- Or a single clean page on your own site or a Notion-style page, as long as it follows the same structure.

## 2. Contents (follow the presskit() structure)
**Factsheet** (left column, quick to scan)
- Game name, developer, platform: "Roblox (PC, mobile, console, VR as supported)"
- Release date / status ("Live", "Early Access", "Launching 2026-12-05")
- Price: "Free to play on Roblox", plus a summary of monetization (be honest)
- Game link: a **Share Link** tagged `press_kit`
- Socials and contact email (a dedicated press address)

**Description**
- One-liner (≤ 15 words), short paragraph (50 words), long description (150-200 words)
- **Features**: 4-6 bullets, verbs first

**Media**
- **Trailer** (YouTube link plus a downloadable MP4)
- **6-10 screenshots** at 1920×1080, clean (no UI or nameplates), plus 2-3 with UI showing the real HUD
- **GIFs** of 3-6 s for each key mechanic
- **Logo** (transparent PNG, light and dark versions) and **icon** (512×512)
- Key art and team photo (optional)

**Credibility**
- Milestones (visits, MAU tier, Developer Awards crowns), awards, selected coverage and creator videos
- Team and credits: names or handles, roles
- Licensed music or partner credits, if any

## 3. Roblox-specific rules
- **Roblox branding:** only the **"Now on Roblox" badge** may be used in your off-platform promotion, following its usage guide. Don't use the Roblox logo or "tilt" as your own branding, and say "on Roblox", not "official Roblox game". See [[Policy - Using Brands, the Roblox Name and Licensed IP]].
- **Screenshots must be real.** Avoid edits that misrepresent the game. The same standard Roblox applies to page thumbnails and videos keeps press and players from feeling misled.
- Show **avatars respectfully**: no other players' usernames or identifiable avatars without consent. Use your team's or friends' avatars.

## 4. Pitch email to press (and bigger creators)
```
Subject: [Game] - <one-liner> - launching <date> on Roblox
Hi <name>, <one sentence why it fits their coverage>.
<Game> is <one-liner>. Launching <date>; <milestone if any>.
Press kit (trailer, screenshots, logo): <link>
Happy to set up a private server for you to play with us.
```
Send 1-2 weeks before launch or a big update. Gaming sites cover Roblox games mostly when there's a **hook**: record-breaking numbers, an unusual genre, a big update, or a brand collab.

## Checklist
- [ ] One URL, no login, loads on mobile
- [ ] Factsheet + 3 description lengths + features
- [ ] Trailer (link + MP4), 6-10 clean screenshots, GIFs, logo PNGs
- [ ] Share Link `press_kit`, press email
- [ ] "Now on Roblox" badge used correctly; no "official Roblox" wording
- [ ] Updated at each major update
