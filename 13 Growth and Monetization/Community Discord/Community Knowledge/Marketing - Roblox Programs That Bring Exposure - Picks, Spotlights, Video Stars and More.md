# Marketing - Roblox Programs That Bring Exposure - Picks, Spotlights, Video Stars and More

Some of the best "outside" marketing is done by Roblox itself: editorial sorts on Home, DevForum spotlights, app-store features and Roblox's own social channels. Most of these programs only need a form submission at the right moment. Program names change often (Creator Affiliate became Creator Rewards in 2025, for example), so this note lists what the Creator Docs say **as of Oct 2026** and when to apply to each.

## Exposure programs (open, per Creator Docs)

| Program | What you get | Apply when | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Today's Picks on Home** | daily editorial slot on Home | each **notable update**, new launch, seasonal moment | only selected games are notified; re-apply for each big update |
| **Standout Games** | curated Home sort for **novel** games | game has deep mechanics, an unusual visual style, or an underrepresented genre (RPG, strategy, puzzle, shooter) | aimed at games that make people think "that's Roblox?" |
| **Off-Platform Featuring** (via Events) | event may appear on **App Store, Google Play** and Roblox marketing channels | submit an event **≥ 7 days before** it starts | needs complete, accurate event metadata; selection uses DAU growth, playtime, retention, monetization |
| **Trending Events in Experiences** (Charts sort) | chart placement for live events | event active, started ≤ 7 days ago, **≥ 1,000 RSVPs**, public | drive RSVPs ("Notify Me") from your socials before the event |
| **Creator Spotlights** | in-depth DevForum article about you | ≥ 1 year developing; a story other creators can learn from | DevRel reviews weekly; if there's no reply in a month, re-apply in 6-12 months |
| **Creator Events** | host webinars or streams as a Roblox event organizer | you're 16+, comfortable presenting, with a complete Talent Hub profile | good fit for creator-devs; potential highlights on Roblox socials |
| **Developer Awards** | avatar crowns at MAU milestones (100+, 1K+, 10K+ ... ), trophies by play hours | automatic, data-driven; owner 13+ | social-post material ("we hit 10K MAU!") |
| **Brand Developer Directory** | visibility to brands | 4M+ MAU game with 65% like score, a brand build, submitter 18+ | later stage |
| **Roblox Brand Link** | paid brand integrations | invite-only; ideally 500k+ MAU | later stage |

## Video creator programs
- **Video Stars** (influencers.roblox.com): eligibility is long-form 100k subscribers with 25k average views over 6 months, or short-form 100k followers with a 50k average, or livestream 10k followers with 300 average concurrent viewers. Content must be in one of the supported languages. Members must post Roblox content every 6-8 months. Benefits and whether applications are open vary between Roblox pages, so check before applying **(status conflict noted, unverified)**.
- **Creator Affiliate pilot**: **deprecated 24 Jul 2025**, replaced by **Creator Rewards** (audience expansion pays for new or lapsed users brought via your Share Link). See [[Creator Rewards - Designing for Daily Engagement and Audience Expansion]].

## Make your submissions land
1. Apply with an **update**, not a pitch: "Season 2 adds X, launches on date."
2. Have the page ready first: fresh thumbnails, a video, an accurate description, an event created.
3. Line it up with your own socials, so featured traffic arrives at the same time as your TikTok or YouTube push and the spike shows strong retention.
4. Keep a calendar row "Roblox submissions" with the date you applied to each program.

## Pitfalls
- Applying only once. Most of these teams review continuously and say to re-submit for each notable update.
- Weak metrics at the moment of featuring. Featured traffic is judged by retention like any other source.
- Assuming an old program still exists. Check the Creator programs page first.
