# Marketing - Off-Platform Rules - Social Links, Allowed Wording and Promo Rewards

Marketing outside Roblox works in one direction: TikTok, YouTube, X or Discord send people **into** the game. Roblox is strict about the other direction. You cannot put a Discord invite on a sign in your lobby, and a "join our Discord for a reward" popup can get the game moderated. Read this before you plan any campaign. It covers the rules that decide which links, words and rewards are allowed.

## 1. The one legal path off Roblox: Social Links

The Community Standards (Security > *Directing Users Off-Platform*) ban every attempt to send users to an outside site or service **except** the **Social Links** feature on game, group (community) or user detail pages. DevForum, Talent Hub, partnered Moments content and some approved ads are handled separately.

| Fact | Detail (Creator Docs, Social media links) |
|---|---|
| Who can add links | Only creators who **verified their age as 16+** (facial age estimation or government ID). Self-declared 16+ is not enough. |
| Who can see them | Only viewers verified as 16+. Under-16 or unverified viewers see nothing. |
| Link types | Facebook, Twitter (X), YouTube, Twitch, Discord, Guilded, Roblox community. Each type **once**. |
| Where to set | Creator Dashboard > game > **Engagement > Social Links** (link type, URL, title) |
| Group games | Need at least one collaborator verified 16+. If all of them leave, the links are **deleted after 90 days**. |

**Practical consequence:** most of a young Roblox audience **cannot see your Discord link at all**. Your off-platform channels have to find players on their own, through videos, creators and search. Don't rely on the game page as a funnel into Discord.

## 2. What you may say inside the game

Text in the game may **point to** the game page, but may never contain the link itself. The docs give these examples (paraphrased):

- Allowed: "Check out our social links on the game page", "Join our community server, links on the game page".
- Not allowed: a pasted discord.gg invite, an x.com handle URL, or an email address for bug reports.

Pattern for a safe in-game panel: `"Updates & codes: see Social Links on this game's page"`. Never include a URL, a QR code or a spelled-out handle.

## 3. Rewards tied to off-platform activity: be careful

The Community Standards (Roblox Economy > *Promotional Offers*) say that **off-platform commercial promotions bundled with on-platform rewards** must be set up under a contract with Roblox. That is aimed at brand deals, such as "buy our snack, get an in-game hat". Plain promo codes that a dev posts on their own X or Discord are a long-standing community practice. Roblox's text doesn't spell out where the line sits for them **(unverified: no official ruling found for self-run social codes)**. A lower-risk pattern:

- Codes give **small cosmetic or currency rewards**. They are never sold off-platform and never tied to buying anything outside Roblox.
- Don't make players prove a follow, like or join off-platform (you can't verify it anyway, and "follow to unlock" reads as forced off-platform direction).
- Also post the same codes **on-platform** (community announcements, update notes) so the reward doesn't depend on leaving Roblox.
- No contests or raffles with Robux or real-money prizes run through Roblox content. The Standards forbid them unless Roblox is involved.
- Never sell game items for money or Robux off-platform. The Standards treat that as getting around Roblox's payment systems.

## 4. Use Share Links, not raw URLs, everywhere off-platform

Share links are free, unlimited per game and tracked in Analytics > Acquisition. They also qualify new and returning players for **Creator Rewards audience expansion**. Every bio, description and pinned comment should use one. See [[Marketing - Share Links per Channel - Naming, LaunchData Perks and Reading Results]].

## Checklist
- [ ] Owner account age-verified 16+; Social Links set (YouTube, Discord, X, Roblox community)
- [ ] In-game text points to "links on the game page" and contains no URLs or handles
- [ ] Codes are small, free and also posted on-platform; no follow-gating
- [ ] No off-platform sales of game items; no Robux contests
- [ ] Every off-platform link is a Share Link
