# Marketing - Creator Outreach - Finding, Pitching and Briefing Roblox YouTubers

Roblox's own Acquisition guide lists **partnering with creators and influencers** as one of the main external growth levers. As a creator yourself, you have an edge: you know what makes a video easy to make. This note turns that into a process: build a target list, send a pitch that gets read, prepare a creator kit, and track each creator with their own Share Link. For anecdotes on pricing and results, see the linked community note.

## 1. Build the list (30-50 names)
- Search YouTube and TikTok for your **genre plus "roblox"** ("roblox zombie game", "roblox horror new"). Note who posts **new-game showcases**, not only one game every day.
- Tiers: **micro (1k-20k subs)**, **mid (20k-200k)**, **large (Video Stars level)**. Roblox's Video Stars program requires roughly 100k subscribers with a 25k average long-form view count, or 100k followers with a 50k average for short-form, or 10k followers with 300 average live CCV for streaming. Those creators are the big end of the scale.
- Columns: `name, platform, subs, avg views last 10, audience age guess, genre fit, contact (business email / Discord), last contacted, status, share link`.
- **Average views beat subscriber count.** A 15k-sub channel averaging 20k views is better than a 500k channel averaging 5k.

## 2. Pitch template (keep it under 120 words)
```
Subject: New Roblox zombie mall game - early access for your channel?

Hi <name>, loved your "<specific video>" - the part where <detail>.
I make <your channel> and just built DEAD ENDS: hold a mall against zombie waves with friends.
- 30 s gameplay: <unlisted link>
- Why it films well: wave chaos, 4-player co-op, a boss that hunts the loudest player
- For you: early access server, a code for your viewers, and <offer: free / Robux / rev share>
Free-cam + no-UI mode are built in. Happy to play with you on stream.
<your name>, <Discord tag>
```
Rules: personalise the first line, give a link that works without logging in, and say up front what you're offering. Follow up **once** after 5-7 days. Silence is normal.

## 3. Creator kit (one shared folder)
- Game link via **their own Share Link** (`cr_<name>_yyyymm`), plus a private-server link for recording.
- 1-page fact sheet: premise, 3 features, controls, secrets worth showing.
- **Viewer code** (e.g. `NAME2026`) granting a small cosmetic. The code doubles as a tracking signal. See [[Promo Codes - Redeem System With Live Configs and Abuse Guards]].
- Clean B-roll and the logo (for their thumbnail).
- Recording helpers: a creator rank with freecam rights only if you trust them, or a no-UI toggle.
- **Disclosure requirement** in writing if anything of value is exchanged. See [[Marketing - Paid Promo Disclosure - FTC, ASA and Platform Labels]].

## 4. Deal shapes
| Shape | Good for | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Free access + exclusive item | micro creators | still needs disclosure (free stuff counts) |
| Flat fee | mid creators with stable views | pay half up front, half on posting |
| Pay per result | anyone | pay per **share-link play** or per D7-retained player; agree on the metric before |
| Rev share | long-term partners | write it down; mind DevEx and tax terms |

## 5. Measure each creator
After 7 days, compare their link: **users with plays, D7 retention, 7D playtime, 30D revenue per user** (Acquisition, Share Links view) against cost. Keep working with creators whose viewers *stay*, not only the ones with the biggest spike. The community note shows that spikes fade fast.

## Pitfalls
- Sending a buggy build. A creator video of a broken game is worse than no video.
- Audience mismatch (a teen horror creator for a toddler obby).
- No server capacity planned for a video spike. Pre-check `MaxPlayers` and server memory.
