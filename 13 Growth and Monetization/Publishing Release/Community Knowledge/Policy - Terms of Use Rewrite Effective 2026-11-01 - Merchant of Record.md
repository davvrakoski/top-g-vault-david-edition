# Terms of Use Rewrite Effective 2026-11-01 - Merchant of Record

> Not legal advice. Summary of Roblox's "Roblox is Updating our Terms of Use" page, checked 2026-10-25. The new text takes effect on **1 November 2026**. Until then the current Terms apply.

Roblox has published a reorganised Terms of Use that goes live one week after this note was written. It is mostly a plain-language rewrite, but one change matters for how creators think about sales: Roblox now states clearly that it is the **principal distributor and merchant of record** for purchases made through Roblox.

## What the change summary says

Roblox's own summary lists three points:

1. Simpler language and a new structure for both the User Terms and the Creator Terms.
2. A clarified role for Roblox in how UGC reaches players: when a player buys or accesses content on the platform, they get it **from Roblox**. Players and creators do **not** enter a direct transaction with each other. Sections on Robux, payments, tax and creator payment are clarified to match.
3. For players in the **EEA only**, a consumer right of withdrawal was added.

## What "merchant of record" means for a developer (our reading)

- The sale of a pass or developer product is a contract between the player and Roblox. You are paid by Roblox under the Creator Terms; you are not the seller to the player.
- This lines up with how refunds already work: the help article for players says creators cannot give Robux or money refunds; players must contact Roblox Support.
- In the new Creator Terms, by publishing UGC you appoint Roblox as principal distributor and supplier of that content to users. Read that clause yourself if you run a business entity, because it affects tax and accounting treatment (ask an accountant, not this note).
- Exceptions are named: Roblox Commerce (physical goods) and the Creator Store (USD sales via Stripe) follow their own rules.

## Other things to notice in the new layout

- **Who can have an account:** ages 5 and older; under-18 accounts must be created with a parent or guardian, who accepts the Terms for the child. "Parent Linked Accounts" are formally defined.
- **Business users:** if you accept on behalf of a company, you confirm you can bind it.
- **Country appendices** are reorganised: Japan, South Korea, Vietnam and Australia appendices, plus a separate page for the UK, EEA and Switzerland.
- **South Korea appendix:** consumers get a 7-day withdrawal right on unused items, and purchases by a minor can be cancelled if the guardian did not consent. Plan for refunds from that region.
- **Arbitration:** the binding arbitration and class-action waiver now names U.S. and Canadian residents (except Quebec).

## What to do before and after 2026-11-01

1. Read both the current Terms and the new version side by side; note any section numbers your own docs cite (team agreements, revenue-share contracts often quote "Section 4 of the Creator Terms").
2. Update internal docs that say "players buy from us"; the official position is that they buy from Roblox.
3. If you sell to EEA or Korean players, make sure your purchase handling copes with refunds and reversals (see [[Purchase Fulfillment and Receipt Reliability]]).
4. Re-check this page after the effective date for any last edits.
