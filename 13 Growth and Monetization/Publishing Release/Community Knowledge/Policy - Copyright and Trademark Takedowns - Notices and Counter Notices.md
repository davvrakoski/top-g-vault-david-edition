# Copyright and Trademark Takedowns - Notices and Counter Notices

> Not legal advice. Roblox's own IP page says the same and recommends an attorney for real cases. Summary of the Creator Hub "Intellectual Property Guidelines", "Rights Manager" and "EU DSA IP infringement reporting", checked 2026-10-25.

Successful games get copied, and games that borrow too freely get taken down. Roblox runs a U.S. DMCA-style notice system for copyright, a parallel process for trademarks, an EU DSA route, and a self-serve **Rights Manager** for verified rights holders. Knowing both sides lets you protect your game and respond calmly if your own content is hit.

## Infringement myths the page rejects

You can still infringe even if you did not monetise, gave credit, found it online, bought a copy, saw others use it, or believed it was fair use.

## If someone copied your game or assets

**Copyright notice** to Roblox's Copyright Agent (email `copyright_agent@roblox.com`, or post/phone listed on the page). Users over 18 may use the IP Platform. Under-13s need an adult to file. A valid notice contains:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the authorised person.
2. A description of your copyrighted work.
3. A description and location (URL) of the infringing material.
4. Your physical address and email.
5. A good-faith statement that the use is unauthorised.
6. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorised.

Modified or AI-altered copies can still be reported; point to the copied expression specifically. Roblox only acts on content hosted on Roblox.

**Trademark notice** to `trademark_agent@roblox.com` with the mark's registration country and number.

**Rights Manager** (Creator Hub > Intellectual Property > Removal Requests): register as a rights holder, verify ownership, then file requests with up to **250 links** per request. Statuses are Pending, Approved, Rejected; requests filed against you appear under **Claims Against Me**. Requests cannot be edited while under review.

**EU route:** DSA notices go to `dsa-ip-reporting@roblox.com`; appeals within six months; certified out-of-court dispute bodies are available.

## If your content was removed

1. Read the Roblox message and email (link and verify an email on every dev account so you receive them).
2. Decide: was it a mistake, do you have a license, or is it really fair use? If not, fix the content instead.
3. **Copyright counter notice** to the Copyright Agent with your name, address, phone and signature; the removed material and its former URL; a penalty-of-perjury statement of good-faith mistake; consent to the relevant U.S. federal court jurisdiction; and acceptance of service from the reporter.
4. Roblox forwards a valid counter notice to the reporter and generally **restores content within 14 business days** unless the reporter shows they filed a lawsuit.
5. **Trademark removals** are appealed by email to the trademark agent; content is restored if the appeal is valid.
6. You can also contact the reporter directly and ask them to withdraw.

Your name or username may be shared with the other side; Roblox may redact address details if doxxing risk is high. You can file through a lawyer or agent instead.

## Repeat infringers and abuse

Roblox suspends or terminates accounts that infringe repeatedly or egregiously. Knowingly false notices or counter notices are illegal (DMCA section 512(f)) and abusing the process to threaten others is banned. If a dispute turns into harassment, report that separately through Report Abuse.

## Preventive habits

- Keep source files, dated exports and Studio version history to prove authorship.
- Keep an asset ledger: where every texture, sound and model came from and under what license.
- Do not copylock-disable your main place unless you mean to share it.
