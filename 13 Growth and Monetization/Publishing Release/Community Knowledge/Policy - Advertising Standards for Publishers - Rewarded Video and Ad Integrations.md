# Advertising Standards for Publishers - Rewarded Video and Ad Integrations

> Not legal advice. Summary of the Help Center "Advertising Standards" (edited 2026-10-22) and "Advertising Integrations Terms" (effective 2026-05-01), checked 2026-10-25.

Ads on Roblox come in two shapes for a game developer: **Roblox-served ads** (immersive billboards and portals, rewarded video) where Roblox pays you, and **Advertising Integrations**, where you sell a brand deal yourself and place the brand inside your game. Both are governed by the Advertising Standards, and brand deals now need registration, review and a revenue share to Roblox. This note is the rules side; for earnings see [[Immersive Ads, Rewarded Video and Sponsored Games]].

## Publisher integrity rules (Roblox-served ads)

- Meet and keep the Immersive Ads publisher eligibility; never fake it.
- **No ad fraud:** no bots, automated activity or engagement campaigns to inflate impressions; no misclassifying genre or maturity to get more ad delivery.
- **No ad content as fallback** when Roblox serves nothing into an ad unit.
- **No misuse of PolicyService** to show ads to users who must not see them (including under-13s).
- Do not cover required ad disclosures or make ads look like game content; do not reuse Roblox-served ad creative.
- Harassing community members can cost you ad eligibility.

## Rewarded video rules

- Disclose **what the reward is** and **what the player must do** before each video.
- The reward must be **predetermined**: no random items or chance mechanics as rewards.
- Require a **clear opt-in click**; never auto-start or force a video as a progress gate, never take over the screen or avatar movement to push it.
- Grant the reward **immediately** after completion.
- No fake rewards such as "free Robux".
- Use only Roblox's Rewarded Video product, and only for **13+** users.
- No reward mechanics at all that push **under-13s** to engage with any advertising.

## Advertising Integrations (your own brand deals)

From the Advertising Integrations Terms:

- **Eligibility:** public game; you are 13+ (or age of majority); your account is **government-ID verified** with **2FA**; questionnaire completed; you use the **Advertising Integrations Eligibility API**; you register (ID, contact and financial info) and meet publisher eligibility.
- **Prior approval:** submit every asset of the campaign to Roblox before players see it.
- **Disclosure:** clearly tell players the content is an ad, in plain language, using Roblox-native disclosures when available.
- **Gate ineligible users** with Roblox's tools. The Community Standards add: no personalised ads to under-18s.
- **No programmatic third-party ad or measurement calls** from the game, and **no sharing personally identifiable engagement data** with the advertiser.
- **No replacing Roblox ad products:** a billboard-style ad must use Roblox immersive ads unless Roblox authorises otherwise in writing.
- **Revenue share to Roblox from 2026-09-30:** a CPM charge per thousand visits while the integration is live, invoiced quarterly, payable within 90 days; unpaid amounts can be deducted from your Robux, and non-payment can suspend the game. Disputes must be raised within 10 days of an invoice.
- You are responsible for any contests or sweepstakes in the campaign and all legal disclosures.

## Banned ad categories (selection)

Real weapons, gambling, lotteries and sports betting, alcohol, tobacco and vapes, cannabis, diet products, cosmetic procedures, dating, politics, religion, occult services, MLM and "earn money" tasks, financial products (self-serve), malware. Ads aimed at under-18s may not urge kids to buy or nag parents, imply the product gives status or skill, or call prices "only" or "just".

## Off-platform links in ads

Only approved destinations; social links are limited to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, Discord and X.
