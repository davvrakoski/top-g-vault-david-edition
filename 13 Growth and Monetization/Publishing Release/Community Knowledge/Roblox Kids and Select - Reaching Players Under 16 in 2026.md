# Roblox Kids and Select - Reaching Players Under 16 in 2026

Roblox now sorts accounts into three tiers by age: **Roblox Kids** (5-8), **Roblox Select** (9-15) and **Roblox** (age-checked 16+). A new game does **not** reach under-16 players automatically. If your audience is kids (most simulators, obbies, roleplay, tower defense), your launch plan must include the steps below, or you will publish to a fraction of your intended audience and wonder why ads and recommendations underperform.

## What each tier can see

| Tier | Who | Ratings allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Roblox Kids | self-declared or age-checked 5-8 | Minimal, Mild |
| Roblox Select | self-declared 9+ (until age-checked) or age-checked 9-15 | Minimal, Mild, Moderate (9+) |
| Roblox | age-checked 16+ | all except Restricted (18+ verified only) |

Games labeled **Content May Vary** (you answered only the first questionnaire question) reach only age-checked 16+ users.

## Requirements to reach Kids and Select (creator side)

1. Account in good standing.
2. **Verified creator account:** facial age estimation if under 18, government ID if 18+.
3. **2-Step Verification** on the account (for group games, the group owner).
4. **Full Maturity & Compliance questionnaire** with a Minimal, Mild or Moderate result.
5. **Fee or subscription:** a one-time refundable per-game fee, **or** an active Roblox Plus/Premium subscription held for 2+ consecutive months. Not refunded if the game is permanently moderated.
6. **Evaluation:** the game first runs for age-checked 16+ users only; Roblox analyzes "highly engaged players" (account age, play history, spend), runs a safety review, and the game qualifies after **250 unique plays by highly engaged age-checked users within 60 days**. Progress shows in the **Audience Reach** dashboard.

## Speeding it up

- **Ads Manager "Engagement" goal** targets age-checked highly engaged players whose sessions count toward the threshold. Roblox notes a higher cost per play, that campaigns don't auto-pause at the threshold, and that results take several days; if no progress after 3-5 days, fix onboarding first.
- **Expedited review:** a one-time refundable fee of **50,000 Robux per game** for timed launches or off-platform campaigns.
- Organic: many games reach the threshold over time without paying.

## Launch plan for a kids-targeted game

- [ ] Week -2: creator ID/face verification and 2SV done; Plus/Premium running 2 months, or budget for the fee.
- [ ] Week -1: full questionnaire answered for the most mature content in the game (blood, violence level, fear, paid random items, trading).
- [ ] Launch: expect 16+ only at first. Plan your soft-launch metrics for that audience.
- [ ] Track Audience Reach daily; decide on Engagement ads or the expedited fee if you have a fixed date (event, YouTuber video).
- [ ] After qualifying: check content again before each big update; changing questionnaire answers is required when content changes.

## Pitfalls

- Promoting to kids on TikTok/YouTube before qualifying: those players cannot join yet.
- Adding gore or new trading features in an update without resubmitting the questionnaire.
- A "Restricted" rating can't be changed back.
