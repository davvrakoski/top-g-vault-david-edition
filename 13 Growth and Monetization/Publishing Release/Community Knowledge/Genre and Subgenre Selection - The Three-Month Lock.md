# Genre and Subgenre Selection - The Three-Month Lock

Your genre decides which genre sorts in Charts you appear in and tells players what to expect. It also can only be changed **once every three months**, so picking it carelessly at first publish can strand a game in the wrong charts for a whole season. Roblox also reviews genres and can change yours if it looks wrong.

## The documented rules

- Set genre and optional subgenre in Creator Dashboard: **Publish Settings > Game Details**.
- Change at most **once every three months**.
- The game page updates instantly; genre-specific Charts can take a few days.
- Roblox may **reassign** a genre it thinks is inaccurate (email + Settings page notice). You can appeal once via roblox.com/support under "Experience Genre" with your universe ID. If you change the genre yourself while an appeal is pending, the appeal is dropped.
- Genres are **separate from maturity**: answer the maturity questionnaire on content, not on genre.

## Roblox's genre list (2026, summarized)

Action, Adventure, Education, Entertainment, Obby & platformer, Party & casual, Puzzle, RPG, Roleplay & avatar sim, Shooter, Shopping, Simulation, Social, Sports & racing, Strategy, Survival, Utility & other. Examples of subgenres that matter for common Roblox games:

| You make | Genre > Subgenre |
|---|---|
| Clicker / pet / lifting sim | Simulation > Incremental Simulator |
| AFK idle game | Simulation > Idle |
| Dropper tycoon | Simulation > Tycoon |
| Tower defense | Strategy > Tower Defense |
| Brookhaven-style town | Roleplay & avatar sim > Life |
| Animal roleplay | Roleplay & avatar sim > Animal Sim |
| Killer vs survivors | Survival > 1 vs All |
| Escape-the-monster | Survival > Escape |
| Battle royale shooter | Shooter > Battle Royale |
| Co-op wave shooter | Shooter > PvE Shooter |
| Racing | Sports & racing > Racing |

## How to choose

1. **Core loop wins.** Roblox's guidance: pick the genre matching the primary purpose or core gameplay loop, even if several fit.
2. **Pick a subgenre if one fits**; it describes mechanics more precisely.
3. **Check where your players come from.** If a hybrid (tower defense with RPG progression), ask which chart your target players browse and where your thumbnail would stand out.
4. **Match title, thumbnail, description and genre.** A mismatch looks misleading to players and invites reassignment.
5. **Decide before your launch push.** Because of the lock, set it once you're confident, ideally before ads or creator videos go live.

## Pitfalls

- Choosing a big genre just for traffic (e.g. "Shooter" for a hangout with a gun) - players bounce and Roblox may reassign.
- Changing genre right before an event, then being locked for three months.
- Forgetting localized descriptions still describe the old genre after a pivot.
