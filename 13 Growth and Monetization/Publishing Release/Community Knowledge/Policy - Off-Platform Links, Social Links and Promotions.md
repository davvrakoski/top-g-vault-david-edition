# Off-Platform Links, Social Links and Promotions

> Not legal advice. Summary of the Community Standards ("Directing Users Off-Platform", "Promotional Offers", "Contests and Sweepstakes"), the Creator Hub "Social media links" page and the Name and Logo guidelines, checked 2026-10-25.

Every growing game wants a Discord, a YouTube channel and a merch store. Roblox allows them, but only through narrow, age-gated channels. In-game text like "join our discord dot gg slash ..." is one of the most common reasons for moderation of otherwise clean games.

## The core rule

External links are allowed **only through the official Social Links feature**. All other ways of directing players off Roblox are banned, including in-game text, decals, signs, NPC dialogue, codes that require visiting a site, and QR codes.

## Social links: who can add and who can see

- **Adding** links to a game, community or Creator Store asset requires the creator to be **age-checked 16+**. Self-declared 16+ is not enough; the UI is hidden until the check.
- Group games need at least **one age-checked 16+ collaborator**. If all of them leave, the group's links are deleted after **90 days**.
- **Viewing** links requires the player to be age-checked 16+. Younger or unchecked players never see them.
- Links appear **only on the game's details page**, never inside the game.

## What you may say inside the game

The docs allow pointing players to the details page: text like "join our community server, links on our game's page" is fine. Their "not allowed" examples spell out an X handle URL, a Discord invite code, or an **email address for bug reports**. So no URLs, invite codes or contact emails in game, and no inviting players to off-platform contests or rewards.

## Promotions, contests and merch

- **Promotional offers:** selling on-platform items through an off-platform promotion (for example a code bundled with a physical product) requires a **contract with Roblox**.
- **Contests and sweepstakes:** creators may not independently promote contests or chance games with **Robux or real-money prizes**. In-game prizes for in-game events are fine.
- **Commerce:** physical goods may be sold only by eligible developers through **approved Shopify** integrations (Commerce Standards).
- **Merch and branding off Roblox:** never put the Roblox logo, name or Classic Avatars on products without a license. Use only the "Now on Roblox" badge in digital promotion.
- **Influencer and YouTuber deals:** if a creator promotes your game for money, follow the advertising disclosure laws where they live; on Roblox, paid promotion follows the Advertising Standards. Brand placements inside your game are Advertising Integrations.

## Your Discord and other off-Roblox spaces

Roblox's rules do not moderate your Discord directly, but off-platform harassment by you can cost ad eligibility, and serious off-platform misconduct can lead to account action. Treat community spaces as part of your reputation: clear rules, adult moderators, no Robux trading channels (third-party Robux trading is banned).

## Checklist

- [ ] No URLs, Discord invites or QR codes in any in-game text, image or audio
- [ ] Social links added on the game page by an age-checked 16+ team member
- [ ] Giveaways pay in-game items only
- [ ] Off-platform merch has no Roblox marks
- [ ] Paid promotions disclosed as ads
