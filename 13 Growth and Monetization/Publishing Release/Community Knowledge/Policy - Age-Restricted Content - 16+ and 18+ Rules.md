# Age-Restricted Content - 16+ and 18+ Rules

> Not legal advice. Summary of the Help Center article "Age-Restricted Content on Roblox" (edited 2026-10-12, checked 2026-10-25). The old "17+" tier has been replaced by 16+ and 18+ tiers.

Horror, survival and shooter games often want more than an all-ages rating allows. Roblox permits extra content behind age checks, but the rules are strict about **order** (label first, content second) and about what stays banned even for adults. Getting this wrong is one of the fastest ways to lose a game.

## The two tiers

**16+ (age-checked users 16 and older)** covers four kinds of game:

- **Social hangouts** without private spaces (talking is the main activity).
- **Free-form user creation** (drawing boards, canvases).
- **Sensitive issues** as the main theme (parents can manually allow these for younger children).
- **Cross-experience content**, where players can view captures from other Roblox games.

**18+ (age-checked users 18 and older, creator also 18+ and age-checked)** allows:

- Graphic, realistic violence and heavy blood.
- Private spaces or adult settings (bars, clubs), and non-sexual romance such as a quick kiss.
- Depictions of alcohol and intoxication.
- Strong language in game content, and optionally in player chat if the creator enables it.
- Social hangouts with private spaces.
- Extended AI interactions (AI companion as the main purpose, or cross-session AI memory).

## Still banned at 18+

- Animal abuse and torture; **real-world** extreme gore or death footage.
- Depicting or glorifying war crimes, torture, real mass shootings or terrorism.
- Any sexual activity, nudity, sexually suggestive avatars, emotes or settings; seeking real romantic relationships or flirting between users.
- Illegal drugs (including marijuana), drug or smoking paraphernalia, vaping, diet pills and steroids.
- Slurs, and strong language used to harass or in a sexual context.

## The order of operations

1. If you want 18+ content, **verify your own age first** (ID or facial age estimation) so the questionnaire shows the Restricted questions.
2. Complete the **whole** Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire and receive Moderate or Restricted.
3. Only then add the restricted content. Adding it before the label is a violation.
4. When the label turns Restricted, Roblox restarts servers and removes every non-18+ player; Studio drops collaborators who are not age-verified 18+ from Team Create.
5. A Restricted label cannot be lowered later. Plan a separate all-ages game if you need both audiences.

## Storefront stays all-ages

The page is explicit: **titles, descriptions, thumbnails and videos of age-restricted games must be suitable for general audiences.** Your gore lives inside the game, not on the icon.

## Assets have their own limits

Uploaded images, meshes and audio go through asset moderation regardless of the game label. Realistic or excessive violence, blood, crude humour or romance in an asset can be moderated even if the game is Restricted. Keep gore procedural or in-engine where possible, and keep uploaded textures tame.

## Region note

Restricted games are unplayable for players located in, or who created accounts in, some countries (Korea, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye are named). Expect a smaller market.

## Practical pattern for a zombie game

- Keep blood off-colour or brief and violence unrealistic to stay Mild/Moderate and reach 9+.
- If you need dismemberment or heavy realistic blood, commit to Restricted and design the business plan around 18+ only.
- Use separate places or a separate game for any "gore mode" rather than toggles, since the label applies to the whole game.
