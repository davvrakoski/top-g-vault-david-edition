# DevEx Eligibility, Earned Robux and Rejected Requests

> Not legal or tax advice. Summary of the DevEx Terms of Use (edited 2026-06-08), the DevEx help page and the Creator Hub DevEx pages, checked 2026-10-25. Rates below are as published on that date.

The Developer Exchange (DevEx) is the only legal way to turn Robux into real money. It is a program you **qualify for on every request**, not a one-time approval, and Roblox reviews how each Robux was earned. Teams that understand the rules before launch avoid frozen cash-outs later.

## Eligibility (all required)

- Age **13+**.
- At least **30,000 Earned Robux** in the account.
- A **verified email**.
- A valid **DevEx portal** account (Tipalti; invitation arrives after the first approved request).
- **IRS form W-9** (U.S.) or **W-8** (non-U.S.) on file.
- Meet the **Ongoing Service Requirements**: keep your content compliant and updated for security, work with Roblox and users to fix Terms issues, and help with legal requests such as removing content accused of copyright infringement.
- Full compliance with the Terms of Use and Community Standards at all times.

Use your **legal name** only. Nicknames, a parent's or friend's name, or joke names can make the account ineligible or delay review. A name mismatch rejection means: do not resubmit, contact Support (ID verification likely).

## What counts as Earned Robux

Counts: in-game sales (developer products, passes, subscriptions, private servers, paid access), Marketplace and Creator Store sales, Creator Rewards, ad revenue, affiliate fees.

Does not count: bought Robux, gift cards, subscription stipends, trading or resale, Robux received as a transfer, passes on template games with no real visits, and money from content later moderated. The DevEx dashboard shows an **estimate** only.

## Rates (2026-10-25)

| Rate | Per Robux | 30,000 Robux |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (earned since 2025-09-05 10:00 PT) | $0.0038 | $114 |
| Old (earned before that) | $0.0035 | $105 |
| U.S. 18+ rate (from 2026-06-08, eligible games) | $0.0054 | $162 |

Order of cash-out: the 18+ rate balance first, then all "old rate" Robux must be cleared before "new rate" Robux pay out. Spending Robux does not reduce the old-rate balance. Group payouts carry the rate of when the group earned them.

The **18+ rate** covers developer products, passes, subscriptions and private servers bought by **U.S. players age-verified 18+**, in games where player characters are R15 or qualifying custom characters **100% of the time** (no R6, R15-only avatar setting).

## Process and timing

Creator Hub > Finances > **Cash Out**. Robux are held during review and refunded if rejected. First-timers must finish portal registration within **one week**. Estimates: about 10 business days for first-time, 5 for returning participants, plus bank time. Max one cash-out per month. Transaction fees come from the payout provider. Accounts inactive 2.5 years must re-apply.

## Why requests get rejected

- Portal registration or tax form not completed in time.
- Not enough Earned Robux.
- Terms violations: scamming, phishing, false advertising, account theft, selling Robux outside DevEx.
- Misrepresenting age or location to obtain special rates, or self-dealing through alt or group accounts.
- Past approvals guarantee nothing; each request is reviewed fresh.

## Tax reminders

Roblox treats DevEx payments as payment for services. U.S. payees get a 1099; non-U.S. payments are not reported to home countries, but **you** must report them. Minors' income can affect parents' returns.
